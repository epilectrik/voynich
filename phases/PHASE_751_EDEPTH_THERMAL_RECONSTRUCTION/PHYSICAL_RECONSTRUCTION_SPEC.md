## 1. The pre-registered prediction (ordinal, NOT scalar)

The grammar is categorical (C469; C287–C290: no scalar encoding). It therefore makes **no
absolute-temperature claim** — pre-registering "edy = 78 °C" would be a category error. The
sharpest claim the grammar can make is a **direction across one physical boundary**:

> The ~100 °C **water-bath ceiling**. A *balneum mariae* (vessel in a boiling-water bath) is
> physically capped near 95–98 °C inside the vessel. A sand/ash bath or direct fire has no such
> cap (routinely 150–300 °C). This is the one absolute thermal landmark a period operator had
> (water boils, sand does not cap) and a modern thermometer trivially reads.

**PREDICTION (locked):** A folio's e-depth class predicts which heat configuration its faithful
reconstruction *requires*, across the water-bath ceiling:
- **HIGH e-depth (gentle-predicted)** → process completes at ≤ ~100 °C (water-bath-compatible:
  distillation of volatiles, gentle digestion).
- **LOW e-depth (intense-predicted)** → process *requires* > 100 °C (direct fire: sublimation,
  calcination, fixation) and **fails** in a water bath.

**KILL (one clean inversion):** a HIGH-e-depth folio whose faithful reconstruction *requires*
super-boiling temperature, **OR** a LOW-e-depth folio that completes fully in the water bath.
A single clean inversion falsifies the e-depth↔temperature ordinal.

**CONFIRM:** across ≥2 folios per class, HIGH reconstructs ≤100 °C and LOW requires >100 °C,
with **no** inversion.

## 3. THE BINDING PRECONDITION (currently UNMET — read before building anything)

The test **cannot discriminate yet**, and the reason is the same structural problem that has
dogged every channel: **reconstruction requires a recipe identity, and we only have confident
content matches for gentle/mid folios.**

- Confident matches exist for **f103r↔III.16 (HIGH/gentle)** and **f75r↔Ch19 (MID, rectification)**
  — both at or above mid e-depth. Both are gentle-or-mixed processes.
- **No LOW-e-depth folio currently has a confident content match** to a process that physically
  *requires* > 100 °C. The intense contrast arm has no recipe to reconstruct.

Without the intense contrast, the test is a **floor**: demonstrating that a gentle-assigned folio
distills at ≤ 100 °C proves nothing (gentle processes are gentle regardless of whether e-depth is
thermal). This is the identical floor-vs-discriminator trap as the cold-read saga — a test that
can only fire its positive arm is evidentially empty.

**GATING REQUIREMENT (pre-registered):** Before the physical test is worth running, obtain **≥1
LOW-e-depth folio** (from the frozen LOW set, §2) confidently matched to an external recipe whose
process *requires* super-boiling temperature (calcination / sublimation / fixation). The match must
clear the anchor-confidence bar (corpus-rare structural conjunction, not 8D-matcher top-1, which is
generic per C2052). Until this is met, **do not build the apparatus** — the test is underpowered by
construction (one-armed).

This precondition is itself a well-defined research task and the correct next computational step if
this direction is pursued: search the frozen LOW set for a corpus-rare match to a high-temperature
operation, with the same composition-matched / corpus-rarity discipline as C2034.
