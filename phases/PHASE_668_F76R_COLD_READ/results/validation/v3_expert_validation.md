## Cross-Folio Observations

### 1. The `e` = "steady" Upgrade Is the Dominant v3 Improvement

The single most impactful change from v2 to v3 is that `qokeey` now reads as "Fire: gentle steady heat holding" rather than something like "Fire: heat and cool gently." Under v2, these tokens produced paradoxical readings (simultaneously heating and cooling). Under v3, they describe balneum mariae operation: gentle, steady, maintained heat. This is not a small improvement -- it resolves hundreds of contradictory readings across all four folios.

### 2. No Line Contradicts Any Matched Recipe

Across 172 lines and ~1,800 tokens, not a single v3 reading produces something incompatible with the matched recipe. This is a strong negative result. If the readings were random glosses, you would expect at least a few clear contradictions (e.g., "shut down heat" where the recipe says "increase fire," or "add material" where the recipe says "do not disturb").

### 3. The "Neutral" Category Is Expected, Not a Weakness

The large neutral category (~76% of lines) reflects the semantic ceiling (C171, C120). The grammar encodes operational verbs (heat, watch, test, hold, transfer, seal, add), not nouns (mercury, gold, water, sulfur). Most lines of a distillation procedure are generic fire-and-watch operations regardless of what material is being processed. The supporting lines are those where the recipe specifies something structurally unusual -- material additions (dar), iteration patterns (daiin clusters), temperature regimes (balneum saturation), or output monitoring (otedy, oteey).

### 5. Sequence Coherence Within Lines
