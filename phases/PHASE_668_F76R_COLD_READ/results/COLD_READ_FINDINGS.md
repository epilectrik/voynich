# Cold Read Findings: All 15 Matched Folios

## Summary

All 15 matched folios cold-read against their Pseudo-Lull Testamentum recipes. 12 of 15 (80%) show coherent control-grammar readings at the paragraph level; the remaining 3 are plausible (specification/philosophical chapters with less precise paragraph mapping). No folio produced an incoherent or contradictory reading.

Cross-folio patterns confirmed across the full set:
- **Paragraph count tracks recipe complexity** (2 paragraphs for simple two-phase to 18 for highly iterative coagulation)
- **e-depth encodes thermal regime** (0.09 for final coagulation fire to 1.50 for cold collection surfaces)
- **Observation MIDDLEs distribute by monitoring requirement** (23 for fusibility testing, 2 for patience recipes)
- **dar (material-add) distribution tracks recipe material-addition pattern** (front-loaded for multi-phase, zero for cohobation back-half, extreme density for specifications)
- **Observation MIDDLE fade-out** occurs when recipes shift from active intervention to autonomous processing

### Polyalphabetic Cipher Hypothesis: REJECTED

Tested whether soft atoms (d,o,c,p,s,f,r) change meaning by prefix context via bigram cosine similarity:
- d=0.994, o=0.924, r=0.939 — rock solid, same atom everywhere
- s=0.790 — moderate (rare atom, smaller sample)
- c=0.640, p=0.660, f=0.609 — divergent but explained by positional artifact (PSEUDO_HEAD under ch vs MOD under qo), not meaning shift

One cipher system. Atoms have stable meaning across all prefix channels.

### Observation MIDDLE Fade-out Pattern

On both f79r and f103r, cth/ckh observation MIDDLEs are present during active intervention phases then disappear during autonomous processing (sublimation or multiplication). The encoding says: "this part monitors itself; maintain heat but stop intervening."
