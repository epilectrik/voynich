> Quant hauràs sublimat e presa la pura substancia del mercuri, adonchs fixaràs la una part de aquell. E quant aquella part serrà fixada, fixarà aprés l'altra. Donchs, reitera la sublimació de la partida no fixa sobra la cosa fixa, en tro que aquella semblantment sia fixa. La qual cosa temptaràs assaiant si bona fusió prestarà sobre lo foch. Reiterate sublimation of the unfixed over the fixed until both are fixed. **Test** by trying if it gives good fusion over fire. If yes — done. If not — add exuberated quicksilver and reiterate until fusible. For a more complex version: dissolve another mercury in the first water, separate by distillation, reiterate distilling and redistilling over feces until all moisture is absorbed. This is the wax-like moisture that withstands fire.
