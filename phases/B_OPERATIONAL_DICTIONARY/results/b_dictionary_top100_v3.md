**Design principles applied:**
1. `e` defaults to "steady" not "cool" -- only use "cool" for explicit temperature reduction
2. Drop vacuous "done" from compound readings -- `-y` is grammatical, not temporal
3. `qoky` = "fire set" not "cease heating" -- the fire stays on
4. `lol` = "equipment steady" not "furnace at rest" -- equipment maintains state
5. Atom chains are not readings -- mark unresolved with atoms in parentheses
6. `a` = "bring to" not "yield" in mid-token -- reserve "yield" for line-final al/ar
7. Consistent prefix labels: Fire/Test/Watch/Vessel/Output/Steady/Load/Scaffold
