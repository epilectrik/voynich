# PHASE_781 — The head of the page: do the first lines of the herbal pages co-vary with their drawings?

**Status: IN PROGRESS. Pre-registration v2 (after a lean-expert design audit, LOCKABLE AFTER EDITS; A1–A8 and B1–B7
incorporated) tagged `phase781-prelock` before Stage 1. Stage 1 (Brunschwig go/no-go) running.**

- **Question.** On the 91 hand-1 herbal pages (Currier A) coded blind in PHASE_780, do pages whose drawings are similar
  carry similar first lines (H1) and first words (H2), beyond position, quire, sheet, layout, spelling dials, the
  initial gallows, style and length?
- **Why.** PHASE_780's positive control (Brunschwig 1500) showed that a real herbal's text–picture link runs mostly
  through plant names at the head of each entry.
- **Design.** Stage 1 runs on Brunschwig only, at 91 entries, the Voynich first-line lengths and the Voynich page
  structure for its nulls. If its genre power is below 0.85 the phase stops and the Voynich first lines are never
  tested against the drawings. Stage 2 calibrates on the Voynich (drift, writing sessions, planted names) and re-checks
  power at the final threshold. Stage 3 is one locked run.
- **Family.** Second of the two planned text–picture tests on these pages (after C2098); not independent of it.
