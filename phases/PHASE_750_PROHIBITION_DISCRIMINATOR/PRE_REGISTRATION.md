**HONEST SOFTENING (Context-First, against crazy's clean story):**

  class-level compliance). Not hard walls.

  residual (FQ_CLOSER boundary tokens + the phase-ordering structure) is unexplained-by-wiring.
- So the discriminating signal is the **RESIDUAL** safety structure (phase-ordering dominance +
  the categorically-safe operation + above-composition hazard), NOT the full 17 transitions.
  We verdict on the residual, not the raw count.
