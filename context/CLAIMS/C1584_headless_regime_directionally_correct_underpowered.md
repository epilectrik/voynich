# C1584: Headless folio regime effect on plant containment is directionally correct but underpowered

**Tier:** 3
**Phase:** 563 (VIRTUAL_APPARATUS_COUPLING)
**Scope:** B, virtual apparatus, headless, containment, underpowered, C1488, C1574, C1523

## Claim

Headless folio regime effect on plant containment/infrastructure metrics is directionally correct (high-headless mean C=0.630 > low-headless 0.589; S: 0.844 > 0.747) but statistically underpowered at N=7 pilot folios (Mann-Whitney p=1.00 for C, p=0.48 for S). Full corpus run needed for adequate power (N>=20 per group).

## Evidence

- P5 headless regime: direction correct, p>0.05
- High-headless folios (N=3): mean C=0.630, mean S=0.844
- Low-headless folios (N=4): mean C=0.589, mean S=0.747
- Mann-Whitney: C p=1.00, S p=0.48
- Sample split: 3 vs 4 folios (hopelessly underpowered for parametric or rank tests)
- Consistent with C1574 (headless ecology folio-parameterized not paragraph-specific)
