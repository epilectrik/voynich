# C1503: Bridge atom redistribution across A and B contexts

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** GLOBAL, A->B, MIDDLE, atom, bridge, redistribution, suffix, PREFIX, HEAD, context
**Phase:** CROSS_LAYER_ATOM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 538)
**Dependencies:** C1140, C1139, C1347, C282, C283

## Constraint

The 85 bridge MIDDLEs undergo systematic morphological redistribution when appearing in A vs B contexts. Bridge HEAD distribution shifts (JSD=0.0767): in A, o-HEAD dominates (28.1%) with depleted e/k; in B, e-HEAD dominates (31.1%) with depleted o. Suffix and PREFIX distributions shift even more dramatically.

Key redistributions:
- Suffix -edy: 0.1% in A, 8.0% in B (~50x B-enriched)
- PREFIX ct: 3.6% in A, 0.3% in B (~12x A-enriched)
- PREFIX qo: 9.1% in A, 17.5% in B (~2x B-enriched)
- PREFIX da: 10.8% in A, 5.0% in B (~2x A-enriched)
- Suffix bare: 64.0% in A, 57.8% in B (A-enriched)
- Suffix -ain: 0.7% in A, 3.2% in B (~5x B-enriched)

Bridge total token counts: 9,391 in A contexts, 19,998 in B contexts.

## Evidence

Top 5 A-enriched PREFIXes:
- ct: 3.6% A vs 0.3% B (12x)
- da: 10.8% vs 5.0% (2.2x)
- do: 1.9% vs 0% (A-only)
- kch: 1.3% vs 0.2% (6.5x)
- dch: 1.5% vs 0.3% (5x)

Top 5 B-enriched PREFIXes:
- qo: 17.5% vs 9.1% (1.9x)
- ok: 6.8% vs 5.9% (1.2x)
- lk: 2.0% vs 0% (B-only)
- sa: 1.5% vs 1.2% (1.3x)
- lch: 1.5% vs 0% (B-only)

Top 5 B-enriched suffixes:
- -edy: 8.0% vs 0.1% (~50x)
- -ain: 3.2% vs 0.7% (4.6x)
- -al: 2.8% vs 1.2% (2.3x)
- -am: 0.8% vs 0.5% (1.6x)
- -ar: 3.3% vs 1.9% (1.7x)

This extends C1347 (B reshapes bridge category usage) to the atom and morphological level: the SAME bridge MIDDLEs are wrapped in dramatically different PREFIX+SUFFIX frames depending on system context.

## Falsification

Would be falsified if bridge tokens showed identical morphological wrapping in A and B contexts (HEAD JSD < 0.01, suffix/PREFIX distributions matching).
