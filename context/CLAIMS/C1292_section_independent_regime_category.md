# C1292: Section-Independent Category-REGIME Association

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B
**Phase:** CATEGORY_REGIME_INTEGRATION (Phase 456)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

The category-REGIME association survives section control. Within-section stratified chi-squared: combined chi2=216.4, dof=49, p<1e-20. All three testable sections show significant within-section REGIME-category differentiation: H (chi2=67.4, p<0.001), S (chi2=116.9, p<0.001), C (chi2=32.1, p=0.004). Section is not the sole mediator of the category-REGIME link.

## Architecture

- **Section and REGIME are partially confounded** (C1029 section-parameterized grammar). If the category-REGIME link were entirely mediated by section, within-section analysis would show no association. It does, eliminating section as the sole pathway.
- **Combined with C1291:** The association survives section control but not kernel residualization. The causal pathway is: kernel composition -> both categories AND REGIMEs, with section as a parallel but insufficient mediator.

## Provenance

- Extends C1029 (section-parameterized grammar) with within-section REGIME-category analysis
- Complements C1291 (kernel mediation) by ruling out section as alternative mediator
- Connects C1282 (category-section prediction) with REGIME structure
