# C1149: Vocabulary Balance Is Orthogonal to Dynamical Archetypes

**Tier:** 2
**Status:** Active
**Scope:** B, cross-system, dynamics
**Phase:** 410 (FOLIO_BALANCE_CHARACTERIZATION)

## Finding

The bridge/dark-pipeline ratio (C1146) is completely orthogonal to the 6 dynamical archetypes (C1016). ARI = -0.002, chi-square p = 0.807. The vocabulary balance axis (how much a folio invests in identification vs grammar) is architecturally independent of the dynamical axis (how forgiving or brittle its control program is).

Spearman correlation between dark/bridge ratio and AXM self-transition: rho = 0.001, p = 0.991. The AXM attractor strength is identical across all three balance groups (mean AXM: BRIDGE_DOMINANT 0.657, BALANCED 0.662, DARK_DOMINANT 0.670; Kruskal-Wallis H = 0.068, p = 0.967, eta² = 0.002).

### Cross-tabulation: Balance × Archetype (n=72)

| Archetype | BRIDGE_DOM | BALANCED | DARK_DOM | n |
|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|---|
| 1 (strong attractor) | 3 | 6 | 1 | 10 |

No archetype preferentially selects bridge-dominant or dark-dominant folios.

## Evidence

- Phase 410, Test 2: ARI = -0.002 (balance labels vs archetype labels)
- Phase 410, Test 2: Chi-square = 6.10, df = 10, p = 0.807
- Phase 410, Test 3: Kruskal-Wallis H = 0.068, p = 0.967 (AXM across balance groups)
- Phase 410, Test 3: Spearman rho = 0.001, p = 0.991 (ratio vs AXM continuous)
- Within-section controls: HERBAL_B p=0.460, BIO p=0.139, RECIPE_B p=0.161 (all non-significant)

## Implication

The material plane (what a folio identifies) and the control plane (how it runs its program) are architecturally independent. A folio can be dark-dominant (identification-heavy) regardless of whether it runs a strong-attractor or active-interchange program. This extends C1016's finding that archetypes are orthogonal to REGIMEs (ARI=0.065) to a third axis: vocabulary balance is also orthogonal to both.

The C1035 irreducible residual (57% of AXM variance) remains unexplained by vocabulary balance.
