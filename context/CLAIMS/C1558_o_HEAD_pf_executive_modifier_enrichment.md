## Claim

o-HEAD selects a distinctive modifier profile dominated by executive atoms p and f while depleting iteration atom i. Within o-HEAD MIDDLEs (2,717 tokens): p enrichment 3.51x (9.7% rate), f enrichment 2.83x (2.7% rate), c enrichment 1.42x (13.1%), s enrichment 1.26x (3.1%), d depletion 0.55x (10.6%), i depletion 0.32x (6.1%). The p and f atoms were identified as "o-HEAD arrangement-affiliated modifiers" in C1543 (Phase 545). This phase confirms and quantifies: p at 3.51x is the strongest modifier enrichment for any single HEAD, and f at 2.83x is second-strongest. The i-depletion (0.32x) is consistent with C1205 (i orthogonal to energy system) and with o's non-iterative arrangement function. This profile directly contrasts with a-HEAD (where i dominates at >50%) and e-HEAD (where d dominates at 38%).

## Interpretation

o-HEAD's modifier profile confirms it as an arrangement/configuration domain. p (pause/marking) and f (flag/marking) are the MARKING-affiliated modifiers (C1390, C1392), while i (iteration) belongs to the iteration axis (C1205, C1207). Arrangement configurations require marking and flagging (p, f) but not iteration (i). The modifier profile is functionally coherent with the domain semantics.
