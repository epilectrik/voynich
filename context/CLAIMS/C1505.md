# C1505: Dark pipeline MARKING-dominant category profile

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, A->B, MIDDLE, atom, dark, pipeline, category, MARKING, bridge, balanced
**Phase:** CROSS_LAYER_ATOM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 538)
**Dependencies:** C1254, C1264, C1140, C1137, C1475

## Constraint

Bridge and dark pipeline MIDDLEs have categorically divergent category profiles. Bridge MIDDLEs distribute balanced across all 8 categories (V=0.4427, chi2=73.31, 82/85 classified): TRANSITION 19.5%, FLOW 17.1%, STAGING 13.4%, THERMAL 12.2%, MARKING 11.0%, OPERATION 9.8%, CONTAINMENT 8.5%, MONITORING 8.5%.

Dark MIDDLEs are MARKING-dominant (36.0%) with secondary FLOW (20.2%), THERMAL (18.8%), and depleted TRANSITION (2.1%), CONTAINMENT (0.7%), MONITORING (2.1%). 292/300 dark MIDDLEs classified (97.3%).

This confirms C1264 (bridge vs dark category divergence) at the full atom-decomposition level and extends C1254 (dark pipeline category generalization): dark vocabulary is specialized for MARKING and FLOW, consistent with its role as identification/labeling vocabulary (C1137).

## Falsification

Would be falsified if bridge and dark had identical category distributions (chi2 NS), or if dark MARKING rate fell below 20%.
