# C1499: Atom ontology is manuscript-wide shared substrate

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** GLOBAL, MIDDLE, atom, cross-system, substrate, Jaccard, HEAD, TERMINAL, modifier
**Phase:** CROSS_LAYER_ATOM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 538)
**Dependencies:** C1393, C1394, C1395, C1141, C1140

## Constraint

The 18-atom MIDDLE ontology (HEAD+MOD*+TERM slot grammar) is a manuscript-wide shared substrate, not a B-local phenomenon. All pipeline channels (bridge, dark, A-exclusive, B-only, AZC) use the same atom inventory with minimum pairwise Jaccard similarity of 0.895. HEAD distribution JSD between non-bridge channels averages only 0.0197 (near-identical). TERMINAL distribution JSD between non-bridge channels averages 0.0046. Modifier distribution JSD between non-bridge channels averages 0.0042. The alphabet is universal; only slot PROPORTIONS differ across channels.

## Evidence

| Channel pair | Atom Jaccard | HEAD JSD | TERM JSD | MOD JSD |
|-------------|-------------|----------|----------|---------|
| bridge-dark | 0.895 | 0.0237 | 0.0822 | 0.0745 |
| bridge-a_exclusive | 0.895 | 0.0377 | 0.0689 | 0.0870 |
| dark-a_exclusive | 1.000 | 0.0117 | 0.0047 | 0.0054 |
| dark-b_only | 0.900 | 0.0106 | 0.0018 | 0.0054 |
| a_exclusive-b_only | 0.900 | 0.0222 | 0.0050 | 0.0069 |
| all_A-all_B | 0.950 | 0.0086 | 0.0019 | 0.0038 |
| all_A-all_AZC | 0.950 | 0.0347 | 0.0029 | 0.0018 |

All channels share 18 core atoms. Bridge has 18 (missing f, has g); dark has 18 (missing g, has f); A-exclusive has 18; B-only has 20 (adds g, x); AZC has 20 (adds g, x). The minimum Jaccard (0.895) occurs between bridge and dark/a_exclusive -- the most functionally divergent channels share >89% of their atom inventory.

Bridge is the JSD outlier across all three slot types (TERM JSD to others: 0.039-0.082; MOD JSD: 0.074-0.088), indicating bridge MIDDLEs have a distinctive slot composition while using the same atoms.

## Mechanistic Explanation

C1141 established that 96.5% of dark pipeline compounds contain at least one bridge atom. C1499 extends this: the entire MIDDLE vocabulary across all four systems (A, B, AZC, HT) draws from a single shared atom substrate. The slot grammar (HEAD+MOD*+TERM) applies universally -- what varies is the PROPORTION allocated to each slot, not the atoms available for each slot.

## Falsification

Would be falsified if any channel used atoms absent from all other channels, or if Jaccard similarity between channels fell below 0.70, indicating system-specific atom inventories.
