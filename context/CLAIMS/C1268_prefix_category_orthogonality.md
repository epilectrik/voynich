# C1268: PREFIX Track and Category Track are Orthogonal

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** A
**Phase:** A_CATEGORY_SCATTERSHOT (Phase 452)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

ch-prefixed and sh-prefixed tokens in A appear alongside identical operational category distributions. Chi-squared=6.52 (8 categories), p=0.480, Cramer's V=0.021, JSD=0.0002. Category contexts of ch-lines (8,669 tokens) and sh-lines (5,473 tokens) are statistically indistinguishable. The prefix track and category track are independent organizational axes.

## Architecture

- **Prefix choice does not select category context.** A record with a ch-prefixed token is no more likely to be THERMAL than a record with an sh-prefixed token, despite B-side evidence of ch/sh functional distinction (C929).
- **Confirms C410 scope.** The ch/sh distinction is frequency- and section-modulated (C410) but not category-modulated. In A, prefixes serve a structural selection function orthogonal to operational theme.
- **Four independent dimensions.** A records are organized by: (1) category (C1261), (2) atom profile (C1265), but NOT by (3) B-mode (C1267) or (4) prefix family (C1268). These are separate organizational axes.
