# C1293: Categories Discriminate Beyond Role Profiles

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B
**Phase:** CATEGORY_REGIME_INTEGRATION (Phase 456)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

Categories add discriminating resolution beyond role profiles for REGIME differentiation. Category JSD exceeds role JSD in 5/6 REGIME pairs. After residualizing category fractions on role composition, Fisher combined KW p=7.5e-8 (4/8 categories individually significant at nominal level). Mean JSD: role=0.0145, category=0.0228. N=72 folios.

## Architecture

- **Categories are not a relabeling of roles.** C545 established role-level REGIME profiles. C1293 shows categories capture additional REGIME-discriminating information that roles miss. The category system (derived from Brunschwig glosses and atom plurality) provides a genuinely different lens than the ICC class/role taxonomy.
- **Orthogonal information axes.** Role composition captures functional grammar structure (what tokens DO in sequences). Category composition captures operational theme (what domain of work tokens DESCRIBE). Both predict REGIMEs, but through different structural pathways.
- **Category > role for 5/6 pairs.** Only REGIME_2 vs REGIME_3 shows role JSD exceeding category JSD. For all other pairs, categories provide sharper REGIME discrimination.
