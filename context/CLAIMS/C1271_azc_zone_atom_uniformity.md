# C1271: AZC Zone Atom-Level Uniformity

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** AZC
**Phase:** AZC_CATEGORY_SCATTERSHOT (Phase 453)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

AZC positional zones (R, C, S, P) do NOT differentiate at atom level. Kruskal-Wallis per AXIS cluster across zones: only 1/8 axes at p<0.05 (FREE, H=10.83, p=0.0127), none surviving Bonferroni correction (p<0.00625). This contrasts sharply with A sections (C1266), where 5/7 AXIS clusters differentiate H/P/T at Bonferroni level.

## Architecture

- **AZC category structure is coarser than A's.** A organizes at both category (C1261) and atom (C1266) levels. AZC organizes at category level (C1269, V=0.084) but NOT at atom level. Category-level zone differences are not driven by atom-level composition shifts.
- **Zones share the same atom distribution.** Despite zones partitioning vocabulary by category bias (C1269), the character-level (atom) composition of vocabulary in each zone is indistinguishable. Category specialization happens at the MIDDLE level, not the character level.
- **A sections differentiate where AZC zones do not.** A's H/P/T sections have strongly different atom profiles (C1266, STABILITY H=30.2, ENERGY H=20.2). AZC zones draw from a common atom pool.
