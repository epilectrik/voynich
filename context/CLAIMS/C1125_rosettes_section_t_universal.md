# C1125: Rosettes Universal Section T Correlation

## Finding

All 9 rosettes correlate most strongly with Section T (text-only) vocabulary. Section T Jaccard ranges from 0.083 (NW) to 0.183 (CENTER), consistently 2-3x higher than the next-best section for each rosette.

**Qualification (C1133):** This section-level correlation is a vocabulary-size artifact. Section T has only 1 non-Rosettes folio (f66r, 112 MIDDLEs), giving it the smallest union denominator. Per-folio analysis shows f66r ranks #11/76; the top 10 overlapping folios are 9 Section S + 1 Section H. See C1133 for full decomposition.

## Evidence

- Per-rosette MIDDLE sets compared against per-section MIDDLE sets via Jaccard
- B corpus sections: H (Herbal), B (Bio), S (Stars), T (Text), C (Cosmo)
- Test X2 in Phase 402 battery

## Implication

The section-level T correlation is a vocabulary-size artifact (C1133). The Rosettes foldout functions as a general-purpose vocabulary reference hub that indexes diverse, vocabulary-rich folios (primarily Section S), not Section T specifically. CENTER rosette has strongest T correlation (0.183) at the section level, but this is driven by T's small union denominator.
