# C1489: Headless Pseudo-HEAD Category Differentiation

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, atom, category, pseudo-HEAD, domain
**Phase:** HEADLESS_COMPOUND_SUBGRAMMAR (Phase 536)
**Date:** 2026-03-06

## Statement

In headless compounds, the initial atom acts as a PSEUDO-HEAD with strong category selectivity (Cramer's V=0.511, chi2=5872, p=0.0). Six pseudo-HEAD atoms define functional domains: d=CONTAINMENT 84.0% (805 tokens), i=STAGING 66.9% (918 tokens), p=MARKING 91.7% (157 tokens), f=MARKING 90.9% (44 tokens), r=FLOW 60.7% (122 tokens), c=OPERATION 32.2% (612 tokens, weakest -- multi-category). The headless aggregate is categorically distinct from ALL 5 HEAD domains (nearest HEAD: o at JSD=0.302, farthest: a at JSD=0.665). Headless enriches CONTAINMENT 10.6x, MARKING 5.4x, MONITORING 4.0x, STAGING 2.9x vs headed; depletes THERMAL 0.11x, FLOW 0.24x, TRANSITION 0.22x.
