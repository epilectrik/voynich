# C1495: HEAD-set atoms do not function as domain selectors when displaced

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, displaced, HEAD, category, pseudo-HEAD, domain, prediction
**Phase:** DISPLACED_HEAD_GRAMMAR (Phase 537)
**Dependencies:** C1489, C1493, C1494, C1475

## Constraint

When HEAD-set atoms {a,e,o,k,t} appear at non-initial positions in headless compound MIDDLEs, they do NOT function as domain selectors. The first atom (pseudo-HEAD) predicts operational category 2.68x more accurately than the displaced HEAD atom (35.1% vs 13.1%, N=1,084). 0/5 displaced HEADs share the same dominant category as their canonical HEAD counterpart. Displaced tokens are MORE similar to same-pseudo-HEAD genuine headless tokens (mean JSD=0.510) than to same-HEAD canonical tokens (mean JSD=0.529). The "displaced HEAD" label is a misnomer: these are genuinely headless compounds containing HEAD-set characters in non-HEAD roles.

## Per-HEAD Category Divergence

| HEAD | Canonical dominant | Displaced dominant | JSD |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|-----|
| k | THERMAL (70.6%) | OPERATION (40.8%) | 0.574 |
| t | FLOW (65.1%) | MONITORING (46.4%) | 0.582 |
| e | OPERATION (36.7%) | MARKING (70.5%) | 0.657 |
| a | FLOW (55.4%) | MARKING (34.9%) | 0.457 |
| o | STAGING (38.3%) | MARKING (32.2%) | 0.376 |

## Evidence

- Pseudo-HEAD accuracy: 35.1% (380/1,084 correct)
- Displaced HEAD accuracy: 13.1% (142/1,084 correct)
- Both correct simultaneously: only 3 tokens (0.3%)
- Neither correct: 565 tokens (52.1%)
- Verdict: PSEUDO_HEAD_DOMINATES

This confirms that the HEAD+MOD*+TERM grammar (C1393, C1394) requires HEAD at position 0. There is no alternative MOD+HEAD+TERM compositional order. When HEAD-set atoms appear elsewhere, they function as modifiers or terminals per their dual-role properties (C1478 for k/t, C1209 for positional grammar).

## Falsification

Would be falsified if displaced HEAD atoms predicted category better than or equal to pseudo-HEAD atoms, or if displaced HEADs matched canonical HEAD category dominants.
