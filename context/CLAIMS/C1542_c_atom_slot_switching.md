# C1542: c-atom slot-switching between PREFIX and MIDDLE

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, PREFIX, atom, c, slot-switching, HEAD, headless, e-HEAD, C1389, C1496, C1542

## Claim

c has dramatically different HEAD co-occurrence profiles depending on whether it appears in PREFIX or MIDDLE position in B. In PREFIX, c selects e-HEAD at 61.0% (as ch/sh modifier channels). In MIDDLE, c concentrates in headless compounds at 46.4% and distributes broadly across HEAD domains (e 17.9%, o 16.8%, k 11.8%, t 5.8%). This slot-switching is unique among MOD atoms and identifies c as the bridge between PREFIX parameterization and MIDDLE execution.

## Evidence

c HEAD co-occurrence in B by slot position:

| Position | n | e-HEAD | headless | o-HEAD | k-HEAD | t-HEAD | a-HEAD |
|----------|---|--------|----------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| PREFIX | 4,608 | 61.0% | 21.2% | 12.0% | 1.8% | 0.8% | 3.2% |
| MIDDLE | 2,096 | 17.9% | 46.4% | 16.8% | 11.8% | 5.8% | 1.3% |

JSD between PREFIX and MIDDLE HEAD profiles = HIGH (e-HEAD shifts from 61% to 18%, headless shifts from 21% to 46%).

c in MIDDLE appears overwhelmingly as medial position (60.7%), consistent with modifier role. Top MIDDLEs: ck (197), kch (148), ckh (127), ct (95), eck (90) -- c modifies HEAD atoms k and t in MIDDLE position.

## Methodology

Phase 545 analysis. HEAD co-occurrence computed for all B tokens where c appears in PREFIX field vs MIDDLE field. N: PREFIX=4,608, MIDDLE=2,096.

- Explains C1496 (c-modifier primary displacement context 87.1%): c bridges PREFIX to headless MIDDLE
- Refines C1389 (c-atom main-loop modifier profile): c's function changes by slot
- Extends C1494-C1498 (displaced HEAD grammar): c+k and c+t are c-mediated terminal constructions
