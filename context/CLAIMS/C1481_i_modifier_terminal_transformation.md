# C1481: i-Modifier Terminal Transformation within a-HEAD

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, atom, i-modifier, a-HEAD, terminal, transformation, category, FLOW, TRANSITION
**Phase:** I_MODIFIER_PARADOX (Phase 534)
**Date:** 2026-03-05

## Statement

Within the a-HEAD domain, i-modification produces a COMPLETE TERMINAL TRANSFORMATION. Non-i a-HEAD tokens are r/l-terminal dominant (r=43.8%, l=33.7%, m=11.2%) mapping to FLOW category (78.1%). i-modified a-HEAD tokens are n-terminal dominant (n=82.1%, bare=16.6%) mapping to TRANSITION category (66.2%). This represents a 70x increase in n-terminal fraction (82.1% vs 1.2%) and an 84x decrease in r-terminal (0.5% vs 43.8%). The category shifts from FLOW-dominant (78.1%) to TRANSITION-dominant (66.2%). Line position shifts earlier (0.536 vs 0.627). i does NOT protect by avoiding high-hazard terminals — 82.9% of i-tokens land in nominally high-hazard terminals (n is HIGH per C1448 at 65.6%). Instead, i changes the CATEGORY of the operation: a→n without i = 100% CONTAINMENT (hazardous), a→n with i = 66.5% TRANSITION + 33.5% FLOW. The protection is CATEGORICAL, not positional.

## Evidence

- **N within a-HEAD:** 1,528 i-modified, 1,551 non-i
- **Terminal shift (r):** non-i 43.8% → i 0.5% (84x decrease)
- **Terminal shift (l):** non-i 33.7% → i 0.3% (112x decrease)
- **Terminal shift (n):** non-i 1.2% → i 82.1% (70x increase)
- **Terminal shift (m):** non-i 11.2% → i 0.0% (eliminated)
- **Category shift:** FLOW 78.1%→28.8%, TRANSITION 16.1%→66.2%
- **Hazard rate:** non-i a-HEAD = 79.3%, i-modified a-HEAD = 28.8%
- **Line position:** i = 0.536, non-i = 0.627 (i is more medial)
- **Line-final rate:** i = 8.6%, non-i = 21.8% (i avoids line-final)
- **a→n frame detail:** i hazard 33.5% vs non-i hazard 100.0% (p<0.001)
- **PREFIX distribution:** Both ok/ot-dominated; i slightly more ok (22.3% vs 18.5%)

## Falsifiable Prediction

If i-modified a-HEAD tokens were reassigned random terminals from the non-i a-HEAD terminal distribution, their hazard rate would rise to ~79.3% (the non-i rate). The terminal transformation is the necessary mechanism for i's protective effect within a-HEAD.
