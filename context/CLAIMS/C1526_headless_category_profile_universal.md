## Claim

Headless compounds share a near-identical operational category profile across all three systems (cross-system JSD=0.023-0.035), characterized by near-zero THERMAL (0.6-1.1%) and near-zero OPERATION (1.1-4.5%), with high STAGING (30.9-35.2%) and MARKING (12.8-19.5%). The headless-vs-headed category divergence within each system is large (JSD=0.226-0.317) but the headless profiles ACROSS systems are very similar. This confirms headless compounds perform the SAME structural function regardless of system: infrastructure/identification, never thermal execution. The cross-system headless category JSD is 6.7-13.8x SMALLER than the within-system headless-vs-headed JSD, proving that the headless functional signature transcends system boundaries.
