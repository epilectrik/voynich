# C1497: Displaced HEAD extreme suffix rate

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, displaced, HEAD, suffix, rate, morphology
**Phase:** DISPLACED_HEAD_GRAMMAR (Phase 537)
**Dependencies:** C1492, C1494, C1440

## Constraint

Displaced-HEAD headless tokens have a suffix attachment rate of 89.8%, dramatically higher than both canonical headed compounds (35.7%, 2.51x) and genuine headless compounds (24.0%, 3.74x). This is the strongest morphological signature distinguishing displaced from genuine headless tokens. Per displaced HEAD atom: k=96.8%, t=95.4%, a=92.6%, e=85.4%, o=63.6%. Per pseudo-HEAD atom: c=96.3%, p=94.0%, y=90.0%, i=90.5%, l=83.8%.

## Connection to Terminal Opacity

The high suffix rate connects directly to C1440-C1445 (terminal opacity gradient). Displaced-HEAD tokens are overwhelmingly bare-terminal (83.9% of headless bare-terminal tokens are displaced, C1498). Bare-terminal is the most TRANSPARENT tier (89.0% suffix rate, C1440), which explains the elevated suffix attachment. The genuine headless population, dominated by n-terminal (OPAQUE, 0.4% suffix) and y-terminal (OPAQUE, 0.3% suffix), has suppressed suffix rates.

## Evidence

| Population | Suffix rate | N |
|------------|------------|---|
| Displaced-HEAD headless | 89.8% | 1,182 |
| Canonical headed | 35.7% | 12,841 |
| Genuine headless | 24.0% | 2,130 |

This suffix rate aligns with the PARAMETRIC vs BINARY distinction (C1492): displaced-HEAD tokens behave like c/p/f-initial parametric operations (>93% suffix), not like d/i-initial binary operations (<15% suffix).

## Falsification

Would be falsified if displaced-HEAD suffix rate were similar to genuine headless (24%) rather than dramatically elevated.
