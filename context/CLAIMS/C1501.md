# C1501: Bridge terminal tier outlier

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, A->B, MIDDLE, atom, terminal, bridge, dark, pipeline, LOCKED, CHANNELED, DIFFUSE, outlier
**Phase:** CROSS_LAYER_ATOM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 538)
**Dependencies:** C1487, C1440, C1140, C1434, C1139

## Constraint

Bridge MIDDLEs are the terminal-tier outlier across all pipeline channels. Bridge has the highest LOCKED terminal rate (8.2% vs dark 3.7%, a_exclusive 3.8%, b_only 4.6%), the highest CHANNELED rate (23.5% vs dark 6.0%, a_exclusive 8.5%), and the lowest bare rate (58.8% vs dark 74.7%, a_exclusive 76.7%, b_only 74.6%). Bridge m-terminal is enriched 3.76x over B baseline (4.7% vs 1.25%), and h-terminal 1.69x (9.4% vs 5.6%).

Dark MIDDLEs show the opposite pattern: highest bare rate (74.7%), highest DIFFUSE/h rate (15.7%, 2.82x B baseline), lowest CHANNELED (6.0%), near-zero y/n terminals (1.3% and 0.7%).

TERM JSD between bridge and all other channels (0.039-0.082) is 5-20x larger than JSD between non-bridge channels (0.002-0.012). Bridge is the structural outlier, not dark.

## Evidence

| Terminal tier | Bridge | Dark | A-exclusive | B-only | B baseline |
|--------------|--------|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| LOCKED (r+m) | 8.2% | 3.7% | 3.8% | 4.6% | 9.7% |
| CHANNELED (y+l+n) | 23.5% | 6.0% | 8.5% | 6.3% | 41.1% |
| DIFFUSE (h) | 9.4% | 15.7% | 11.1% | 14.6% | 5.6% |
| bare | 58.8% | 74.7% | 76.7% | 74.6% | 43.6% |

Individual terminal enrichments (vs B baseline):
- Bridge: m 3.76x, h 1.69x, bare 1.35x
- Dark: h 2.82x, bare 1.71x; y 0.064x, n 0.072x
- Dark CHANNELED depletion: y+n+l = 6.0% vs bridge 23.5%

P3 prediction (dark prefers CHANNELED) FAILED: dark actually avoids all non-bare/non-h terminals. The correct pattern is dark preference for DIFFUSE (h) + bare, not CHANNELED.

## Mechanistic Explanation

Bridge MIDDLEs need category-imposing terminals (LOCKED r,m per C1487) to function as the dynamical backbone -- they participate in transitions and closures that require specific operational category signals. Dark MIDDLEs, as identification vocabulary, prefer maximally transparent terminals (bare, h-DIFFUSE) that don't constrain category, allowing flexible deployment across contexts.

## Falsification

Would be falsified if dark had higher LOCKED or CHANNELED rates than bridge, or if bridge were not the terminal-tier JSD outlier.
