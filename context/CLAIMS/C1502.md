# C1502: AZC o-HEAD domain enrichment

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** AZC, MIDDLE, atom, o-HEAD, HEAD, enrichment, arrangement, C1381
**Phase:** CROSS_LAYER_ATOM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 538)
**Dependencies:** C1381, C1388, C1475, C1269, C1273, C496

## Constraint

AZC MIDDLEs show o-HEAD enrichment at 2.70x (type-level) relative to B baseline (31.8% vs 11.8%). This is the strongest HEAD enrichment of any channel for any HEAD atom. AZC simultaneously depletes k-HEAD (0.314x) and t-HEAD (0.488x) -- the executable domain HEADs -- while maintaining near-baseline e-HEAD (0.909x). AZC headless rate (25.9%) is the lowest of any channel, indicating AZC preferentially selects headed MIDDLEs with o as the dominant HEAD.

Token-weighted AZC HEAD profile: o=22.4%, e=30.8%, a=15.3%, headless=27.9%, k=2.6%, t=1.1%.

This extends C1381 (o-initial MIDDLE enrichment in AZC at token level) to the type level with stronger effect and provides the HEAD-domain framework: AZC is an arrangement-dominated system with depleted thermal/flow capacity.

## Evidence

| HEAD | AZC % | B baseline % | Enrichment |
|------|-------|-------------|------------|
| o | 31.8% | 11.8% | 2.70x |
| e | 27.6% | 30.3% | 0.909x |
| a | 8.6% | 13.3% | 0.644x |
| headless | 25.9% | 27.2% | 0.954x |
| k | 4.2% | 13.4% | 0.314x |
| t | 1.9% | 4.0% | 0.488x |

AZC HEAD JSD to B: 0.0245. AZC HEAD JSD to A: 0.0347. AZC sits between A and B in HEAD space but closer to B.

## Falsification

Would be falsified if AZC o-HEAD enrichment fell below 1.5x, or if k/t showed equal or higher enrichment than o in AZC.
