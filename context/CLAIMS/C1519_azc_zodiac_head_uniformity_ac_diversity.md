# C1519: Zodiac HEAD Uniformity vs A/C Internal Diversity

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** AZC, family, zodiac, ac, HEAD, uniformity, diversity, JSD, C436
**Phase:** AZC_ZONE_ATOMIZATION (Phase 541)

## Claim

Zodiac and A/C AZC families have similar overall HEAD profiles (between-family JSD=0.0158) but sharply different internal diversity. Zodiac intra-family HEAD JSD=0.0617; A/C intra-family HEAD JSD=0.1265 -- A/C is 2.0x more internally diverse. Zodiac folios share a HEAD domain template (uniform scaffolds, C436); A/C folios are individually specialized in their HEAD domain mixtures. This extends C436 (dual rigidity: uniform vs varied scaffolds) to atom-level resolution: Zodiac uniformity and A/C variety are not just scaffold-level properties but penetrate to the HEAD domain composition of their vocabulary.

## Evidence

- Between-family HEAD JSD: 0.0158 (small -- families similar overall)
- Zodiac HEAD profile (N=1823): a=19.3%, e=31.9%, o=20.5%, k=1.9%, t=1.0%, headless=25.4%
- A/C HEAD profile (N=1404): a=10.2%, e=29.3%, o=24.9%, k=3.5%, t=1.1%, headless=31.1%
- Zodiac internal HEAD JSD: 0.0617 (low -- uniform across 15 folios)
- A/C internal HEAD JSD: 0.1265 (high -- varied across 14 folios)
- Diversity ratio: A/C 2.0x more internally diverse than Zodiac
- Terminal JSD between families: 0.0059 (even smaller than HEAD -- families near-identical in terminal closure)
- Zodiac headless: 25.4%; A/C headless: 31.1% (A/C has more infrastructure/headless compounds)
