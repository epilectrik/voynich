# C1496: c-modifier primary displacement context

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, displaced, HEAD, c-modifier, k, t, ck, ct, context
**Phase:** DISPLACED_HEAD_GRAMMAR (Phase 537)
**Dependencies:** C1389, C1478, C1488, C1494

## Constraint

The modifier 'c' is the primary context for HEAD-set atom displacement: 87.1% of c-initial headless tokens contain a displaced HEAD (533/612). c+k accounts for 365 tokens (68.5% of c-initial displaced), c+t for 167 (31.3%). The dominant MIDDLEs are ck (197 tokens), ckh (127), ct (95), cth (49). The compositional pattern MH (modifier+HEAD, 28.9%) and MHT (modifier+HEAD+terminal, 16.4%) together account for 45.3% of all displaced tokens. After c, the next highest displacement rates are q (92.3%, but these are PREFIX characters, not grammar), y (70.4%), s (65.6%), and r (54.9%).

## Mechanistic Explanation

c-modifier (adjust/main-loop, C1389) naturally combines with k/t in their TERMINAL roles (C1478). In ck, the 'c' is the pseudo-HEAD providing OPERATION domain (C1489: c=OPERATION 32.2%), and 'k' serves as terminal. The k atom is NOT functioning as THERMAL domain HEAD but as a terminal marker. This explains why ck's category is OPERATION (40.8%), not THERMAL (which would require k at position 0).

## Evidence

| Pseudo-HEAD | Total headless | Displaced | Rate |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|------|
| c | 612 | 533 | 87.1% |
| q | 104 | 96 | 92.3% (non-grammar) |
| y | 71 | 50 | 70.4% |
| s | 32 | 21 | 65.6% |
| r | 122 | 67 | 54.9% |
| l | 428 | 216 | 50.5% |
| d | 805 | 104 | 12.9% |
| i | 918 | 21 | 2.3% |

Note the extreme split: i-initial (the largest headless population) has only 2.3% displacement, while c-initial has 87.1%. This reflects the different modifier-terminal affinities: i bonds exclusively with n-terminal (C1484), which categorically excludes HEAD-set atoms (C1498). c takes h-terminal (C1484) which is compatible with HEAD-set atoms.

## Falsification

Would be falsified if displacement were evenly distributed across pseudo-HEAD atoms rather than concentrated in c-initial context.
