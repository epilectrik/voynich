### C1072 — Terminal Character Predicts Within-Group Compatibility

- **Tier:** 2 (ESTABLISHED)
- **Scope:** B (MIDDLE compatibility x morphological character)
- **Phase:** MULTI_LAYER_COMPATIBILITY_ARCHITECTURE (2026-02-15)

**Finding:** 5 of 17 terminal-character groups show within-group C475 compatibility rates >2x the 2.17% baseline (p<0.01): 'n' (32.97%, x15.2), 'm' (17.86%, x8.2), 'y' (15.58%, x7.2), 'r' (5.04%, x2.3), 'l' (4.94%, x2.3). INITIAL-biased terminals (p, l, k from C1067) have mean within-group rate 3.71%, while FINAL-biased terminals (c, i, d) have 1.17% — a 3.2x asymmetry.

**Interpretation:** MIDDLEs sharing the same terminal character are significantly more likely to be compatible partners in C475. The strongest signals come from suffix-like characters (n, m, y) that may define morphological subfamilies sharing construction constraints. The INITIAL > FINAL compatibility asymmetry connects C1067's positional grammar to C475's compatibility structure: atoms that tend to appear early in compounds share more compatibility than late-position atoms. Consistent with C985 (character-level features have limited power) — effect is modest in absolute terms but highly significant.

**Extends:** C1067 (terminal character positional bias V=0.231), C475 (MIDDLE incompatibility), C985 (character-level features insufficient for full discrimination)
