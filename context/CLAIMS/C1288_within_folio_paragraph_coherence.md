# C1288: Within-Folio Paragraphs Share Category Profiles

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B
**Phase:** CATEGORY_MECHANISM_DECOMPOSITION (Phase 455)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

Paragraphs within the same folio are more category-similar than paragraphs across folios. Within-folio mean JSD=0.1086 vs null=0.1217, z=-4.92, p<0.001 (1000 permutations). Across-folio mean JSD=0.1223. Within/across ratio=0.888. N=484 qualifying paragraphs (>=5 categorized tokens) across 74 folios.

## Architecture

- **Folio-level category coherence.** The folio (= program, per C976) sets a category theme that constrains all its paragraphs. A THERMAL-heavy folio has THERMAL-heavy paragraphs. This goes beyond C1029 (section-parameterized grammar) to show folio-level thematic unity.
- **Paragraph independence is partial.** C891-C893 established paragraphs as independent operational programs. C1288 qualifies this: paragraphs are structurally independent (grammar, kernel access) but thematically coherent (category composition). Independence is in execution mechanics, not in operational theme.
- **Connects to A-record coherence.** C1261 showed A records have category coherence (d=9.7). C1288 shows B paragraphs inherit folio-level thematic coherence, consistent with vocabulary-mediated correlation from A through AZC to B (C468).
