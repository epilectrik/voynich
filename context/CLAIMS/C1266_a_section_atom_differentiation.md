# C1266: A Section Atom-Level Differentiation

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** A
**Phase:** A_CATEGORY_SCATTERSHOT (Phase 452)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

## Statement

A sections (H, P, T) are differentiated at atom level despite MIDDLE-level uniformity (C946 cosine 0.997). 5 of 7 AXIS clusters show significant between-section variation (Kruskal-Wallis p < 0.00625, Bonferroni-corrected): STABILITY (H=30.2, p<0.001), FREE (H=20.9, p<0.001), ENERGY (H=20.2, p<0.001), MONITORING (H=16.7, p=0.0002), CLOSURE (H=12.9, p=0.002). Section profiles: H is CLOSURE/MONITORING-heavy, P is STABILITY/ENERGY-heavy, T is ITERATION/ENERGY-heavy.

## Architecture

- **Atom decomposition breaks C946's barrier.** C946 found sections indistinguishable at MIDDLE identity level (cosine 0.997). At atom level, 5 of 7 axes differentiate them. The sections were always different -- MIDDLE-level analysis averaged over internal composition.
- **Section profiles match expected domains.** P (Pharmaceutical/plant) is STABILITY/ENERGY-heavy (process stability, energy input). T (Theriac/recipe) is ITERATION/ENERGY-heavy (repeated cycles, energy application). H (Herbal) is CLOSURE/MONITORING-heavy (containment, observation).
- **Two axes are section-universal.** ITERATION (p=0.069) and STRUCTURAL (p=0.276) do not differ across sections, forming a shared operational substrate.
