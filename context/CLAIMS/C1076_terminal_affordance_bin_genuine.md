### C1076 — Terminal Character Predicts Affordance Bin Beyond Frequency

- **Tier:** 2 (ESTABLISHED)
- **Scope:** B (MIDDLE terminal character x C995 affordance bins)
- **Phase:** TERMINAL_COMPATIBILITY_GEOGRAPHY (2026-02-15)

**Finding:** Terminal characters predict C995 affordance bin membership beyond what frequency alone explains. Full contingency: chi2=443.9, V=0.2253. After excluding BULK_OPERATIONAL (566/972 MIDDLEs), non-BULK V=0.3247 (chi2=342.3, p=3.4e-18). Critically, permutation null (shuffling terminal labels, 1000x) gives mean V=0.2102 with perm_p=0.000 — the observed signal significantly exceeds frequency-matched expectation. 10 affordance bins confirmed in data (not 9 as C995 originally described). HUB sub-role analysis shows terminal-specific patterns across 10 terminal groups with HUB MIDDLEs.

**Interpretation:** Unlike roles (C1073) and states (C1074), affordance bins carry genuine terminal-character structure that is NOT fully explained by frequency. This is the first Phase 382 test to survive the frequency-mediation null. Terminal characters appear to encode functional neighborhood membership at the affordance level — MIDDLEs ending in the same character tend to occupy similar functional niches (affordance bins) beyond what their frequency similarity would predict. Notable profiles: 'm' terminal 50% FLOW_TERMINAL (vs 5.5% population); 'e' terminal 22% STABILITY_CRITICAL (vs 4.5%); 'h' terminal 17% PHASE_SENSITIVE (vs 2.8%).

**Extends:** C1072 (terminal compatibility signal), C995 (9+1 affordance bins), C986 (frequency baseline)
**Consistent with:** C1000 (HUB sub-roles), C985 (character-level features have limited power — affordance is the exception)

**Quantitative:**
- Contingency: 17+ terminal groups x 10 bins, 972 MIDDLEs
- Full: chi2=443.9, V=0.2253, p=4.5e-28
- Non-BULK (406 MIDDLEs): chi2=342.3, V=0.3247, p=3.4e-18
- Permutation null: mean V=0.2102, perm_p=0.000
- Bin count confirmed: 10 (FLOW_TERMINAL, ROUTINE_SPECIALIZED, PRECISION_SPECIALIZED, COMPOUND_TERMINAL, BULK_OPERATIONAL, SETTLING_SPECIALIZED, HUB_UNIVERSAL, ENERGY_SPECIALIZED, STABILITY_CRITICAL, PHASE_SENSITIVE)
- HUB sub-role analysis: 10 terminal groups contain 23 HUB MIDDLEs total
