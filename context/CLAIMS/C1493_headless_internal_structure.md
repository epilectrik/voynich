## Statement

The dominant pattern is MT (modifier+terminal, 46.7%), followed by T-only (19.3%), MMT (16.3%), and MMMT+ (5.1%). 35.7% of headless compounds contain HEAD atoms {a,e,o,k,t} in non-initial positions -- these are "displaced HEAD" compounds where a modifier or terminal atom leads, with the HEAD atom functioning as a non-initial element. This mixed population suggests headless is not purely infrastructure but includes compounds where the initial atom's role (e.g., iteration control via i, sealing via d) takes grammatical priority over the embedded operational HEAD. The nearest HEAD domain is o (JSD=0.302), reflecting shared emphasis on STAGING/arrangement; the most distant is a (JSD=0.665), reflecting headless avoidance of FLOW/TRANSITION.
