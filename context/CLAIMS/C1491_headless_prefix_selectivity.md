# C1491: Headless da-PREFIX Near-Exclusivity

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, PREFIX, da, selectivity, sa, ta
**Phase:** HEADLESS_COMPOUND_SUBGRAMMAR (Phase 536)
**Date:** 2026-03-06

## Statement

Headless compounds show extreme PREFIX selectivity. da PREFIX is 2284x enriched in headless (17.8% vs ~0.008% in headed) -- near-exclusive to headless compounds. sa and ta PREFIXes appear EXCLUSIVELY with headless MIDDLEs (sa: 197 tokens, ta: 107 tokens, both 0 headed). Conversely, ok and ot are near-absent from headless (ok: 0.5% of headless vs 7.5% headed, ot: 0.8% vs 7.2%). The da-enrichment is specifically i-initial (C1394 noted da+iin/in pattern). Combined with C1397, this confirms headless compounds occupy a distinct PREFIX channel separated from the main headed instruction space.
