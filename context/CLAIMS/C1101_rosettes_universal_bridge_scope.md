# C1101: Rosettes Universal Bridge Scope

**Tier:** 2 (STRUCTURAL INFERENCE)
**Scope:** ROSETTES
**Phase:** BRIDGE_BACKBONE_MANUSCRIPT_SURVEY (Phase 390)
**Strengthens:** C1098 (structural index), C1089 (near-complete coverage)

---

## Statement

The 10 bridge MIDDLEs absent from the Rosettes (of 85 total bridges) are randomly distributed across manuscript sections (chi2=4.24, p=0.752). The Rosettes does not exclude bridges from any particular section — its 75/85 coverage (88.2%) is a universal sample, not a section-biased one.

---

## Evidence

### Missing Bridges
The 10 absent bridge MIDDLEs: ect, eeol, ka, keed, ock, okc, opc, qo, sh, tcho

### Section Distribution of Missing Bridges
| Section | Missing | Present |
|---------|---------|---------|
| H | 10 | 75 |
| S | 9 | 75 |
| T | 8 | 65 |
| B | 7 | 67 |
| P | 6 | 71 |
| A | 4 | 62 |
| Z | 4 | 61 |
| C | 3 | 58 |

Chi-squared test: chi2=4.24, p=0.752 (non-significant)

### Interpretation
The missing bridges are evenly distributed, appearing in all 8 sections. No section is preferentially excluded. The 10 missing bridges are sampling noise from a near-complete universal coverage, not a systematic gap.

---

## Implication

Combined with C1089 (98% class coverage, 100% hub coverage), this confirms the Rosettes is a **universal** index of the manuscript's bridge backbone. The dual-layer structure identified in earlier phases — universal bridge layer (C1096, C1098) plus pharma-specific tail layer (C1091) — is now quantified: the bridge layer covers 88.2% of all bridges with no section bias, while the non-bridge tail vocabulary overlaps equally with B, T, and S sections (C1100).
