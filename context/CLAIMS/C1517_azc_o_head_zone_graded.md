# C1517: AZC o-HEAD Enrichment Is Zone-Graded Not Uniform

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** AZC, zone, atom, o-HEAD, enrichment, graded, C1502, C1381, C1388
**Phase:** AZC_ZONE_ATOMIZATION (Phase 541)

## Claim

AZC o-HEAD enrichment is ZONE-GRADED, not uniform. o-HEAD fraction ranges from 17.7% in R-zone (1.51x B) to 29.3% in S-zone (2.49x B), a spread of 11.6 percentage points. The overall AZC o-HEAD rate at HEAD-slot decomposition is 22.4% (1.90x B), which is lower than C1502's 2.70x measured at MIDDLE initial-atom level. The discrepancy arises because HEAD decomposition (C1394) excludes headless tokens where o may appear as a modifier, not a domain selector. S-zone and L-zone (labels) drive the strongest o-enrichment (29.3%, 30.9%); R-zone and P-zone are closer to B proportions (17.7%, 19.1%). This extends C1388 (o = arrangement domain marker) with zone-specific deployment: arrangement vocabulary concentrates in S (star/series) and C (center) zones, not R (ring) zones.

## Evidence

- o-HEAD enrichment by zone (vs B 11.8%):
  - R: 17.7% (1.51x B) -- lowest major zone
  - C: 26.2% (2.23x B)
  - S: 29.3% (2.49x B) -- highest major zone
  - P: 19.1% (1.63x B)
  - L: 30.9% (2.63x B) -- labels most o-enriched
  - AZC overall: 22.4% (1.90x B)
- A baseline: o-HEAD = 28.5% (2.42x B) -- AZC is between B and A
- C1502 reported 2.70x using MIDDLE initial-atom (not HEAD slot); the difference reflects measurement methodology, not contradiction
- o appears as both HEAD and MOD in AZC (HEAD 22.4%, MOD 17.8%), never TERM (0.0%) -- consistent with C1388 (o is arrangement domain marker, not terminal)

## Relationship to Prior Constraints

- **Refines C1502**: Confirms o-HEAD enrichment but at 1.90x not 2.70x; difference is measurement methodology (HEAD slot vs initial-atom); zone gradient is the new finding
- **Extends C1381**: o-initial enrichment in AZC confirmed with zone-level resolution
- **Extends C1388**: o as arrangement domain marker concentrates in S/C/L zones
- **Connects C1269**: Zone category specialization now has atom-level substrate -- zones with more o-HEAD should be more STAGING/OPERATION enriched
- **Connects C1507**: A selects o-HEAD/headless (arrangement); AZC S/C/L zones show the same emphasis
