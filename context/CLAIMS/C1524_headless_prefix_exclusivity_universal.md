## Claim

The da-PREFIX near-exclusivity for headless compounds documented in B (C1491, 2,284x enrichment) extends universally to all three systems: A (844.6x), B (1,448.3x), AZC (132.7x). sa and ta PREFIXes are also near-exclusively headless in all systems (A: sa=99.2%, ta=98.6%; B: sa=99.7%, ta=100.0%; AZC: sa=98.2%, ta=94.4%). The da/sa/ta→headless binding is a MANUSCRIPT-WIDE grammar rule, not a B-specific phenomenon. This provides the strongest evidence yet that PREFIX→MIDDLE HEAD selection (C1415) operates identically across all systems.
