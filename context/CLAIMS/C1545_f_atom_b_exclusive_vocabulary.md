# C1545: f-atom anomalous B-exclusive vocabulary affinity

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, atom, f, bridge, dark-pipeline, B-exclusive, vocabulary, rarity, C1139, C1499

## Claim

f has the lowest bridge rate (shared A+B MIDDLEs) of any MOD atom at 49.3%, meaning 50.7% of f-containing tokens in B use B-only MIDDLEs. f is also the rarest modifier atom in B with only 215 tokens (next lowest: s at 560). f participates disproportionately in B's autonomous identification vocabulary (dark pipeline). p also shows elevated B-only rate (34.3%). Together, p/f's bridge rates (49.3% and 65.7%) are lower than d/i (89.4% and 92.8%), indicating p/f are preferentially deployed in B-specific vocabulary.

Bridge defined as MIDDLE type appearing in both A and B systems. B-only = MIDDLE type in B but not A.

f's top MIDDLEs: fch (23), ofch (17), f (17 -- standalone), of (15), cfh (9). Many are short headless compounds specific to B.

Phase 545 analysis. Bridge/B-only classification based on MIDDLE type presence in system token lists. N = all B tokens containing each atom in MIDDLE position.
