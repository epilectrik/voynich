# C1525: Headless Compounds Have Lower Suffix Rate Than Headed Across All Systems

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** GLOBAL, cross-system, headless, suffix, rate, depletion, universal, C1440, C1490, C1492
**Phase:** HEADLESS_CROSS_SYSTEM (Phase 542)

## Claim

Headless compounds have LOWER suffix attachment rates than headed compounds in all three systems: A (35.2% vs 48.0%, ratio=0.73x, chi2=177.0, p=2.2e-40), B (43.1% vs 50.2%, ratio=0.86x, chi2=92.6, p=6.4e-22), AZC (35.9% vs 59.0%, ratio=0.61x, chi2=137.3, p=1.1e-31). AZC shows the strongest effect (0.61x). This is consistent with C1490 (headless terminal profile shifted: h enriched 2.98x, LOCKED r/m depleted 6.2x) since h-terminal is the primary suffix gatekeeper (C1512, 98.7% suffix rate) — headless compounds' h-enrichment (T7: 2.24-2.79x) is insufficient to compensate for their massive bare-terminal depletion (0.56-0.58x headed). Headless compounds AVOID suffix specification, consistent with their role as infrastructure/identification vocabulary rather than executable operations.

## Evidence

- A: headless 35.2% (1533/4353) vs headed 48.0% (3275/6821), ratio=0.73x, chi2=177.0, p=2.2e-40
- B: headless 43.1% (2705/6277) vs headed 50.2% (8446/16819), ratio=0.86x, chi2=92.6, p=6.4e-22
- AZC: headless 35.9% (323/899) vs headed 59.0% (1373/2328), ratio=0.61x, chi2=137.3, p=1.1e-31
- All three systems show the same direction (headless < headed), all highly significant
- AZC strongest effect, B weakest (B's headless compounds are closer to headed in suffix behavior)

- **Refines C1492**: C1492 documented WITHIN-headless suffix bifurcation (d/i-initial=bare vs c/p/f-initial=suffixed). C1525 establishes the OVERALL headless-vs-headed direction: headless is suffix-depleted across all systems
- **Connects C1490**: Headless terminal profile shift (h enriched, r/m depleted). Suffix depletion follows from terminal composition via C1440 (three-tier opacity)
- **Connects C1512**: MIDDLE terminal dominates suffix content selection (V=0.513). Headless terminal shifts mechanistically produce suffix depletion
- **Extends C1440**: Terminal opacity is cross-system. Headless compounds' terminal profile produces suffix depletion through the same opacity mechanism in A, B, and AZC
