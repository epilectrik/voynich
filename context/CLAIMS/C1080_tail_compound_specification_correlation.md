### C1080 — Tail Pressure Predicts HT Compound Rate (Specification Model)

- **Tier:** 2 (ESTABLISHED)
- **Scope:** B (folio-level HT morphology × MIDDLE frequency distribution)
- **Phase:** HT_INTERACTION_ARCHITECTURE (2026-02-15)

**Finding:** Folios with higher tail pressure (more rare MIDDLEs) have significantly higher HT compound rates: Spearman rho=0.367, p=0.0007, n=82 B folios. Partial correlation controlling for folio HT count is even stronger (rho=0.425, p=0.0001), ruling out mechanical artifact. The correlation is POSITIVE, supporting the C935 specification model over the two-axis model (Tier 3).

**Interpretation:** The HTSC cross-guarantee prediction (C477 × C935) reveals a genuine interaction: folios that deploy more rare vocabulary also deploy more compound HT MIDDLEs. This is consistent with C935's view of compounds as operational specifications — folios with more diverse vocabulary require more specific HT markers, producing compound MIDDLEs as specification vehicles. The two-axis model (Tier 3) predicted an inverse correlation (high tail → simple HT morphology), which is rejected. Compound HT MIDDLEs track specification density, not cognitive simplicity.
