# C1490: Headless Terminal Profile Shift

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** B, MIDDLE, headless, atom, terminal, profile, enrichment, hazard
**Phase:** HEADLESS_COMPOUND_SUBGRAMMAR (Phase 536)
**Date:** 2026-03-06

## Statement

Headless compounds show a systematic terminal atom profile shift vs headed compounds. h-terminal enriched 2.98x (16.2% vs 5.4%), n-terminal enriched 2.45x (25.0% vs 10.2%). LOCKED tier (r+m) massively depleted: r 0.153x (1.4% vs 9.1%), m 0.21x (0.3% vs 1.6%), combined LOCKED 1.7% vs headed 10.7%. This represents structural hazard avoidance -- r-terminal is the primary hazard vector (C1447, 92.58% of forbidden violations). The shift concentrates headless terminals in the DIFFUSE (h) and CHANNELED (n) tiers, away from the hazard-carrying LOCKED tier. Terminal-modifier exclusivity rules (C1484) hold in headless: n compliance 100%, h 99.6%, y 99.6%.
