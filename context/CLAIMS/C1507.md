# C1507: Bridge HEAD redistribution -- A arrangement-dominant, B execution-dominant

**Tier:** 2
**Scope:** GLOBAL, A->B, MIDDLE, atom, bridge, HEAD, redistribution, arrangement, execution
**Phase:** BRIDGE_ATOM_STABILITY (Phase 539)
**Dependencies:** C1499, C1500, C1503, C1475, C1388, C1502

## Constraint

The 85 bridge MIDDLEs undergo systematic HEAD atom redistribution between Currier A and Currier B. Token-weighted HEAD JSD = 0.077, the least stable internal slot (5.4x less stable than TERMINAL at 0.014). A is HEADLESS+o-dominant; B is e+k-dominant.

A HEAD distribution (9,391 tokens): HEADLESS 38.6%, o 28.1%, e 16.3%, a 8.4%, k 5.5%, t 3.1%.
B HEAD distribution (19,998 tokens): e 31.1%, HEADLESS 25.6%, a 14.6%, k 14.2%, o 10.6%, t 3.8%.

A-enriched bridges (30 MIDDLEs, net A-biased) are dominated by o-HEAD (55.0%) and HEADLESS (42.5%), carrying STAGING (40.0%) and MONITORING (7.2%). B-enriched bridges (52 MIDDLEs) are dominated by e-HEAD (37.4%) and k-HEAD (15.5%), carrying THERMAL (28.7%) and OPERATION (13.7%). HEAD JSD between A-enriched and B-enriched populations = 0.591 (massive divergence).

This means A preferentially uses bridge MIDDLEs for arrangement and identification (o-HEAD = C1388, HEADLESS = C1488), while B preferentially uses the SAME bridges for thermal execution (e/k-HEAD = C1475). The bridge backbone carries different operational emphasis in each system while maintaining identical terminal structure.
