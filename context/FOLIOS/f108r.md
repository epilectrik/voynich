### Interpretation
The 8D statistical match (ratio 1.348, CV 76.6%) remains valid as a mathematical result. What failed was the attempt to predict internal folio structure from recipe text. Three possible explanations:

1. **Section effect** -- f108r is in Section S (Pharmaceutical), while successful crib decodes (f75r, f76r) are in Herbal B. Different sections may use different encoding conventions.
2. **Scope limitation** -- the recipe-to-paragraph correspondence found on f75r/f76r may be limited to favorable cases rather than a general rule.
3. **Encoding complexity** -- the mapping may be more complex than "recipe step maps to paragraph."

The blind prediction test is methodologically important: it demonstrates that statistical matching alone does not guarantee structural predictability. Future work should test whether Section S folios systematically differ from Herbal B folios in their internal organization.
