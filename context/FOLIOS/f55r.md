### Historical Context
- Poppy (Papaver somniferum) was one of the most important medicinal plants in medieval pharmacy
- **Opium** (dried latex from scored poppy capsules) was used for pain, sleep, diarrhea, cough
- **Poppy seed oil** (oleum papaveris) was extracted by pressing and/or distillation — used medicinally and as a carrier oil
- **Concentrated opium preparations** required repeated distillation to produce purified extracts
- Opium was a key ingredient in theriac, the most expensive compound medicine
- Brunschwig (1500) includes poppy water (Papaver) in his distillation manual
- The product was extremely valuable — opium was regulated by guild authorities
