## Brunschwig Ch28 Detailed Comparison (2026-04-01)

The PL Ch19 text is only 6 lines — too compressed for detailed comparison. Brunschwig Ch28 (lines 6182-6222) describes the same recipe with full operational detail:

1. Take red thick honey, add equal spring water
2. Boil and skim over gentle coals until water consumed
3. Repeat 9 times
4. Boil down until thick as honey
5. Circulate in balneum mariae at first degree for 40 days
6. Distill through alembic in balneum
7. If it rises, wrap cold wet cloths around cucurbit
8. Three fractions: clear water (first), yellow water+oil (second), dark red oil via ash (third)
9. Cohobation: pour first water back over feces, redistill

### What matches at detailed level
- **Iterative cycling (P1-P8):** 4x qokedy run on L13 = enumerated heat cycles. P1-P8 structure consistent with 9x boil/skim.
- **Material addition (L35-36):** Double-dar (unique in corpus) = adding honey + wax (two materials). dar concentrated late in folio where recipe adds materials after initial preparation.
- **Balneum circulation (L41):** Pure sustained gentle heat — every token e-depth >= 1, most BALNEUM. Maps to "circulate in balneum at first degree for 40 days."
- **Quality check (L43):** chekar = TEST exact close. Positioned after balneum and before final processing.
- **Cohobation (L40):** dar AND dal on same line = active material addition + passive transfer. "Pour first water back over feces and redistill."
- **Atom composition validated:** L35 `sheety` = sh+ee+t+y = monitor.cool.cool.transfer.end = "watch extended cooling, transfer off" = SKIMMING while cooling. Precise match.

### What doesn't cleanly match
- **Three fractions:** Multiple output tokens (okar, otar) at L34, L36, L44, L45 but NOT cleanly grouped into three "collect and set aside" blocks. The fractions may be encoded but not as three distinct sections.
- **Balneum-to-ash shift:** e-depth declines L41→L46 (from BALNEUM to gentle/e1) but no dramatic crash to e0 for "distill per cinerem" (third fraction via ash). The ash distillation step is the weakest correspondence.

### Assessment
~80% confirmed at detailed level. Broad operational structure maps cleanly. Three-fraction structure and ash shift are present but not dramatically delineated.

### Atom gloss observations from this comparison
- **a = "yield" preferred over "into"** — ar = yield+respond = "produce output" fits FLOW contexts better
- **n = "bind" preferred over "halt"** — ain = into+iterate+bind = "contained cycling" fits balneum contexts
- **r = "output/result" may be better than "respond"** — ar/or are 99% FLOW category, always at output points
- **t = "transfer" confirmed** — sheety on L35 = monitor.cool.cool.transfer.end = skimming = precise transfer operation
- **e as modifier of k** = "sustain/moderate" (composition rule), not literal "cooling"
