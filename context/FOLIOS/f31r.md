### Brunschwig Rosewater Recipe (from 1500 and 1512 texts)
- **Apparatus:** Glass alembic in balneum mariae (water bath)
- **Heat:** "No hotter than you can well endure a finger in it" (first-degree fire)
- **Pre-digestion:** 3 days in balneum mariae before distilling
- **Distillation:** Gentle, over steam from warm water; coals preferred over wood for fragrance
- **Cohobation:** Pour distillate back over spent roses 1-2 times; "the more often, the nobler"
- **Rectification:** Sun exposure in sand for 40 days; annual re-sunning for 12+ days
- **Quality tests:** 5 methods — taste/smell, thread test, thumbnail test, flake sediment, color change
- **Shelf life:** Red rose water 3 years, white 2 years, wild 2-3 years (all require annual maintenance)
- **Sources:** Brunschwig 1500 lines 327, 2369-2435; Brunschwig 1512 lines 40761-40775, 39663-39680
