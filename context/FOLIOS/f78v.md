## Latin Re-Reading (2026-04-02)

The Latin text reveals 16 distinct operational steps, not the 2-3 phases suggested by the English summary:

1. dissoluendo (dissolve with Mercury water)
2. congelandi (congeal sulfur nature)
3. congeleta (it has congealed)
4. rubificare (rubify/redden)
5. dissoluere (dissolve the rubification again)
6. congelando per plures vices (congeal through several repetitions)
7. misce aquas (mix the waters)
8. fermentatione (small fermentation)
9. euaporatum cum calore (evaporate with heat)
10. ter (three times)
11. **facto in balneo Mariæ continuè** (done in bath of Maria continuously)
12. quousque nihil possit distillari (until nothing more distills)
13. pone sapere cineres (place wisely upon ashes)
14. coopertorio clauso (with cover closed)
15. quousque siccetur humor (until moisture dries)
16. fermentet (let it ferment)

**Balneum appears at step 11 of 16** — it's a LATE step. Steps 1-10 are fire-driven (dissolve, congeal, rubify, evaporate). Steps 13-16 are ash-drying. The 9.2% gentle heat matches: balneum occupies 2/16 steps (12.5%).

**Triple-dal on L2 maps to the Latin:** "per virtutem desiis liquoris, & sulfuris, & miscentes verae dulcedinis" — three components (liquor + sulfur + sweet mixings) placed together. Three passive placements = three dal.
