## Structural refinement to C764/C921 (f57v R2 ring)

The H-track resolves the decades-old "is the ring 4 identical repeats?" question: **NO.** R2 is one
12-glyph sequence repeated across 4 quadrants, **10/12 positions invariant** (the fixed "scale"
`o l d r · x k _ _ · t r · · y c`), **2 positions variable**, read out per-quadrant:

| position | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pos 9 (f/p) | f | f | **p** | **p** | `0 0 1 1` = **high bit of the quadrant index** — a clean hemisphere/parity marker |
| pos 8 (k/m) | k | **m** | k | k | single deviation, Q2 only — anomaly or scribal m/k slip |

This sharpens C764 ("p/f mark ring halves") into a coordinate encoding: the invariant scale gives
within-quadrant position, the f/p bit gives the hemisphere — **f57v R2 is an addressing grid**
(48 stations = 12 scale × 4 quadrants), a drawn **reference dial / protractor** of calendrical/
cosmological *form* (12×4 + half-marker; central figure-group where a volvelle rotor would mount).
*Form only* — the zodiac/seasonal **referent** reading is NOT asserted (it was burned this session;
see `feedback_label_fit_to_signal`). Structure = dial; what it indexes = unrecoverable (C171).

## The synthesis: one authorial single-char reference notation, used three ways

The manuscript carries a single-character symbol-set **distinct from running Voynichese**, drawn
from a shared inventory, appearing on three pages in the SAME (reference) functional class:

| page | object | function | constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **f57v** | coordinate ring (R2) | **addressing / coordinate key** | C763, C764, C920, C921 |
| **f66r** | bold mark column (M) | **register / index marks** (authorial; fluent hapax names alongside) | C1992 (C1993 glossary reading retracted) |
| **f49v** | numbered label column | **a later reader's analysis/imitation** of the notation | F49V_ANNOTATOR_ANALYSIS |

Inventory overlap is high: f57v ring ∩ f66r marks = `c d f l o p r t x y` (10 chars); f57v ∩ f49v =
`d f k o p r y` (7). C762 already records the f49v/f76r/f57v primitive overlap (d,k,o,r); this note
adds **f66r** to the family and supplies the functional reading (addressing vs register vs imitation).

**Critical exclusion — f76r is NOT in this family.** f76r's single-char lines are **operational**
(control-posture sentinels that *propagate into B grammar*, C121/C366/C382/C403; per C497's explicit
contrast), not reference marks. So the single-char phenomenon splits into two functional classes:
- **REFERENCE / non-operational:** f57v, f66r, f49v (this family) — addressing, indexing, imitation.
- **OPERATIONAL:** f76r — grammar-gating sentinels. Do not merge.
