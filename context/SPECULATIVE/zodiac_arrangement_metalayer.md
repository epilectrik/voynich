## The interpretation

> The AZC zodiac section is a **generic arrangement/configuration index** — a kernel-free reference layer in the **Rosettes/AZC family**, **position-independent**, that the Currier-B programs draw on generically rather than by any per-program routing.

## What the measurements support (legible structure)

- **Kernel-free arrangement class.** Atom-HEAD profile sits ~5× closer to the Rosettes than to A or B; kernel-HEAD ≈ 0.01 (C2072, C1819/C757 note). The labels are arrangement-class vocabulary, not execution.
- **Position-independent.** The nymph clock-positions carry no detectable HEAD-encoding (C2071, bounded null); no label↔band angular binding, no angular autocorrelation. Matches the Rosettes "no spatial gradient" (C1128).
- **Generically accessed.** Every B program touches all 12 diagrams; no program concentrates on one diagram/season (C2073). The index is a shared pool, not a routing table.
- **An e-stabilization substrate under a varying label layer.** The band is the most e-enriched, kernel-free population (C2072); the labels are the varying entries over it.

## The wall (why this stays Tier 3 / held)
