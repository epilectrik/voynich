## Summary

The f85-86 rosettes foldout is most plausibly a **medieval alchemical workshop diagram** — a topology of multi-vessel apparatus and material flows around a central heating apparatus, with workshop context at the corners. The text labels are captions on the diagram, written in the metalayer's AZC-like grammar.

This is interpretive synthesis combining independent visual evidence with text-structural findings. **Not text-statistically validated** — Phase 683's retest of the balneum signature claim with corrected methodology failed pre-registered criteria. Stays at Tier 4 ceiling.

## The Synthesis

The workshop-diagram interpretation explains all the structural findings as a coherent whole:

1. **Why metalayer (AZC-like, not B-like):** It's a diagram with text captions, not running prose. The "syntax" is iconographic; the text is labels.

2. **Why path tokens have material markers (da-prefix):** Paths are physical material conduits between apparatus components. da-prefix marks material being conveyed.

3. **Why node tokens have apparatus markers (ok-prefix):** Nodes are apparatus stations. ok-prefix marks apparatus state/correction.

4. **Why CENTER differs structurally:** CENTER is the central heating apparatus (multi-alembic balneum); outer-8 are connections/contexts. Different functional roles.

5. **Why generic indexing across B folios:** The workshop apparatus serves all recipes. All recipes use SOMETHING in this workshop, so all recipes share vocabulary with the rosette labels generically.

6. **Why recipes don't map to specific paths:** Recipes describe HOW to operate (procedures). The foldout shows the apparatus on which to operate (topology). Different conceptual layers — orthogonal information.

7. **Why bigram transitions are random:** Topology diagrams don't have a directional reading order. Like a circuit diagram, you can read in any order; what matters is which apparatus connects to which.

## Limits

This is interpretive synthesis at Tier 4. **It cannot be promoted higher because:**

- Visual identifications (CENTER = balneum specifically; NE = settlement) depend on iconographic reading conventions, not text-statistical validation
- Body-text balneum signature would have provided text-structural anchor; Phase 683 retest failed pre-registered criteria
- Specific identifications of individual rosettes/cardinals/corners are interpretive overlays on insufficient text data
- The synthesis is most coherent reading available, not falsifiable in a strong sense

**Specific limits:**
- The 8-around-1 layout matches "4 elements + their phases + quintessence center" iconographic tradition, but we cannot rule out other interpretations (cosmological mandala, mappa mundi, philosophical framework)
- The Brunschwig comparison is suggestive but Voynich predates Brunschwig (radiocarbon ~1404-1438 vs Brunschwig 1500). Both likely draw from a common 14th-15th c. distillation iconographic tradition
- Whether the diagram represents a SPECIFIC workshop or an idealized/conceptual one is undetermined

## What Would Promote This Above Tier 4

Tier 3 promotion would require:
- Independent text-statistical anchor confirming a specific iconographic identification (e.g., body-text balneum signature exists with corrected methodology — Phase 683 attempted and failed)
- Pre-registered cross-validation against external corpora describing similar apparatus topology
- Some falsifiable structural prediction the workshop reading makes that other readings don't

Tier 2 would require all of the above plus replication on independent visual annotations and across multiple matched corpora.

## Methodological Lessons

1. **Text-only methods have ceilings on visual content.** When primary information is in drawings, text-feature engineering can characterize structural properties (path/node, AZC-like) but cannot decode pictorial content.

2. **Visual annotations are interpretation, not raw data.** They cannot ground constraints by themselves. They can motivate text-statistical predictions; if those fail (Phase 683), the visual interpretation stays at Tier 4.

3. **Pre-registration discipline held.** Phases 680-683 collectively tested 4 specific hypotheses about rosette structure (recipe-class map, recipe-as-transition, da-MIDDLE state encoding, balneum signature). All failed pre-registered criteria. The session produced 1 Tier 3 structural finding (C1989) and 2 Tier 1 falsifications (C1990, C1991), not a Tier 2-3 interpretive claim.

4. **The user's intuition was sharp:** the foldout's visual prominence reflects its functional importance, but its purpose is communicated visually rather than textually. Our methods correctly identified WHAT we were missing (the visual content) but cannot independently validate the iconographic identification.
