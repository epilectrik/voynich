## Four Principal Operations (stated explicitly at f. 99v)

> *"Quat operasiones: disolre; lavar; congelar; fixar"*
> Four operations: dissolve; wash; congeal; fix.

These are the Practica's declared operational framework, stated before any recipe begins.

## Drip-Counting System (Ch9P / f83r)

The Catalan text explicitly describes monitoring distillation by counting drops:

> *"quant destillara a .VI. punts o a .X. o a .XV. o a .XX."*
> "when it distills at 6 drops or 10 or 15 or 20"

Fire is adjusted based on the drip rate:
- At 20 drops evenly → begin flame fire
- If rate drops to 15 and liquid is clear → continue that fire
- If drops to 12 or less → reduce fire
- Then strengthen fire by one degree beyond the descent → continue until nothing more distills

This maps to f83r's heavy **ch prefix** (17% — active testing/counting) and the e_depth gradient encoding fire degree adjustments.

## Key Insight: Catalan Is More Operationally Explicit Than Latin

Where the Latin abstracts, the Catalan preserves workshop-level detail:

| Latin | Catalan | Additional detail |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| *fortificare ignem* | *fortifica ton foc de carbo poc a poc* | Names fuel type (charcoal) + rate (little by little) |
| *pone in balneo* | *posa-la tot en bani calt per tres dies* | Specifies duration (3 days) |
| *distillare* | *quant destillara a .XX. punts* | Specifies drip-count monitoring |
| *lutare* | *llutaras la junctura com benas de drap liniddas ab pasta feta de flor de farina destrenpada ab blanc de ou* | Names luting material: linen bandages + flour paste + egg white |
| *cave a magna igne* | *guarda't de masa foc, que ab calor de sol avem vist que s'es fet* | Adds that "with sun's warmth we have seen it done" (successful temp) |
| — | *ve't a dormir* | "Go to sleep" (explicit overnight pause) |

The Voynich atom system encodes at the Catalan level of specificity — fire degree, apparatus type, monitoring mode, timing. The Latin translation flattens this granularity. This suggests the Voynich scribe worked from a source closer to the Catalan than to the Latin tradition.
