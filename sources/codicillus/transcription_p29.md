# Codicillus Page 29 — Cipher Key Diagram

## Letter-to-Substance Mappings (all red ink)

**B** (primo primo = "first of the first"):
- aurum (gold)
- argentum (silver)
- materia vilis et filis (vile/cheap material)
- mercurius (mercury)

**G** (secunda prima = "second of the first"):
- ignis (fire)
- lux (light)
- aqua (water)
- terra (earth)

**M** (tertia prima = "third of the first"):
- Sulfer (sulphur)
- argentum vivum (quicksilver)
- [?]mentus (possibly pigmentus or fermentus)

**P**:
- Medicina
- Chirurgia (surgery)

**V**:
- Multiplicatio
- eiusque gradus et [?]limatio (and its degrees and sublimation)

## Graduated Scales

| Letters | Domain | Meaning |
|---------|--------|---------|
| c d e f | aparos / Graduatio de b | Graduation degrees of B |
| g h i k l | Media de igne | Medium/grades of fire |
| n o p q | Media de aqua | Medium/grades of water |

## Heat Modes (bottom section)

- Calores fortissimi (strongest heats)
- ignis flatus studio (forced blast fire)
- Calor balnei (heat of water bath)
- lignis separatos (separated/sorted woods)

## Marginal Symbols

- calor [?]esta — heat of...
- ignis siccus — dry fire
- balneus — water bath
