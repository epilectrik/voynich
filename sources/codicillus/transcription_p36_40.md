# Codicillus Pages 36-40 — Degree Tables, Cipher, Master Wheel

## Page 36: Degree Scales

### Scale 1: "Scala gradus primorum qualitatum in magisterio"
(Scale of degrees of primary qualities in the magisterium)
- Four columns for four degrees
- Each degree rates: calefaction, humidity, frigidity, dryness
- Mapped to elements: ignis, aer, aqua, terra

### Scale 2: Operational grade letters l, m, n, o, p, q
- Each letter has graduated operational effects
- l = liquefaction first degree
- Through q = advanced operations

### Scale 3: Letters T, R, O at bottom

## Page 39: Complex Geometric Diagram

Large composite figure: master circle containing squares, triangles, 
and 8 smaller circles at cardinal/intercardinal positions.

Eight perimeter circles labeled with elements:
- terra (earth) at top
- aqua (water) at left
- ignis (fire) at right and bottom-right
- aer (air) at bottom

Center: overlapping squares and triangles with letter combinations.
Caption: "2 figura quae extrangulari... triangulus quod personnt signum circuli"

## Page 40: Master Wheel (Rota)

Large circle with inscribed equilateral triangle. Cross at top.

### Triangle vertices:
- Top: terra / sulphur
- Bottom-left: aqua / umbra
- Bottom-right: ignis / aer

### Center: "materia grossa" (gross matter = prima materia)

### Outer ring: elements distributed around circumference
- terra, aqua, aer, ignis with associated qualities

### Corner annotations (four qualities):
- Top-left: Ignis, Calidus, Siccus, Subtilis (Fire, Hot, Dry, Subtle)
- Bottom-left: "prima elementatio, sulphur" (primary elementation)
- Bottom-right: "terra mundida, gelatio" (purified earth, congelation)

### Bottom substance table:
terra / sulphur / medicina
aqua / aurum / 
ignis / dies et ignis / fusibilis

### Final: "materia grossa" repeated — everything returns to the prima materia
