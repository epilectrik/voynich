# Codicillus Pages 31-32 — Elemental Figures & Apparatus

## Page 31: Triangles, Elements, Sulfur Classification

### Three Triangles (top row)
- Left: circle with {red: a}
- Center: circle with {red: II}
- Right: circle with {red: RE}
- Text references "triangularis" figures

### Square with Four Element Circles
- Top-left: terra (earth)
- Top-right: aqua (water)
- Bottom-left: aqua/aer
- Bottom-right: ignis (fire)/aer
- Center: "a parte de b" — relating to the B cipher letter

### Sulfur Classifications (bottom)
- **Sulfur rubeum simplex** — simple red sulfur (= Testamentum cipher C)
- **Sulfur rubeum** [compositum] — composite red sulfur (= cipher F)
- **Sulfur album** — white sulfur
- Note: "tres tam divisiones quam quatuor divisiones" — three and four divisions

## Page 32: Elemental Layers & Apparatus

### Concentric Circles (elementated forms)
Post-distillation elemental composition:
- Outermost: aqua elementata (processed water)
- Second: terra elementata (processed earth)
- Third: aer et vinum elementatum (processed air and wine)
- Innermost: ignis elementatus (processed fire)

### Flask Drawing
- **"lambicus magistri"** — the master's alembic
- Long neck with curved spout, round body with dots (material inside)

### Sulfur + Medicine Section
- Sulfur discussion continues
- **medicina** introduced as the goal
- **aurum** (gold) referenced
- Two small circles at bottom with rubeum and gnano labels

## Key Finding
The concentric elemental layers show a PHYSICAL MODEL of distillation:
fire at core (the active principle), surrounded by earth, air, and water
in successive layers. This is what distillation progressively separates.
