# Codicillus Page 30 — Rota Alphabetalis (Lullian Wheels)

## Layout: ~9 circular diagrams in roughly 3x3 grid, all red ink

### Top Row — Three Figures
1. **prima figura** — concentric circles, letters a.b.c.d., labels: signalis, negationis, figura
2. **2a figura** — concentric circles, letters a.b.c.d., label: affirmationis
3. **3a pars** — with "deus" (God) labeled nearby

### Middle Row
4. **Large M** at center — Gothic capital M, outer ring letters b.S.a., quarta figura
5. **Overlapping circles** (vesica piscis) — activa/passiva, materia, fiabilis
6. **Sectored circle** — Sapientia (wisdom), concordia, materia

### Bottom Row
7. **Circle with h** — "Circulus digestionis" (Circle of digestion) labeled below. Cross symbol.
8. **Circle with Ω/U** — sectors with letters A.D., divided into compartments
9. **Small circle** — ymmanuel, bonus deus — Christological/theological key

## Key Features
- All text red ink — diagram-only page
- Theological terms (deus, ymmanuel, sapientia) mixed with natural philosophy (digestio, materia, humiditas)
- Activa/passiva distinction in overlapping circles
- "Circulus digestionis" explicitly identifies alchemical context
- Letter stations (A, B, C, D, S) at positions around wheels
