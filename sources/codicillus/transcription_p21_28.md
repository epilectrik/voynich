# Codicillus Pages 21-28 — Pre-Cipher Text + Colophon

## Section Headers (all red ink)

1. **p.22:** "De modo faciendi ultimam decoctionem" — On making the final decoction
2. **p.23:** "De operatione chymicae" — On the alchemical operation
   - "Balneum rubeum et..." — Red bath and...
3. **p.24:** "De forma circulorum totius perfecti" — On the circles of perfection
4. **p.25:** "Bona de solutio" — Good dissolution
5. **p.26:** "De proprietatibus" — On properties (of three and one perfection)
6. **p.27:** "De 2a quod concernit ad Mercurio et Luna" — On Mercury and Moon
7. **p.28:** "Explicit" — End marker

## Colophon (Page 28, bottom)

{red: Explicit} ... in saecula saeculorum
amen sequenti die epiphaniae
anno A° m° cccc° lxx [?] ... qui scripsi

{red: Deo gratias}

**DATE: January 7, 1470** (day following Epiphany)
Scribe attestation: "qui scripsi" (who wrote this)

## Content Notes

Pages 21-28 are dense Latin prose with heavy abbreviation. The text
discusses practical methodology:
- Final decoction procedures
- Red bath (balneum rubeum) operations  
- Mercury and Moon (silver) operations
- Circle/wheel application methodology
- Dissolution procedures

Key vocabulary: aqua, distillatio, medicina, ignis, terra, gelatio,
sublimatio, rubedo, balneum, Mercurius, Luna, alambicum, vinum, phlegma

## Note on Readability

This is the hardest section to transcribe — extreme abbreviation,
faded ink, and cursive hand. Section headers in red ink are most reliable.
The colophon date (1470) is the single most important finding.
