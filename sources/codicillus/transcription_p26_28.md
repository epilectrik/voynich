## Image 008 (Page 28) — Ownership Inscription

Centered ownership inscription in a later hand (18th century), with decorative star/asterisk marks above and below:

```
         *
Bonaparte Carolos. [monogram/symbol]
A. Dom. 1713  Advocij Cu[riae?]
         *
```

**Notes:**
- "Bonaparte Carolos" — an owner named Carlo Bonaparte (or Carolus Bonaparte)
- "A. Dom. 1713" — Anno Domini 1713, the date of acquisition or inscription
- "Advocij Cu[riae?]" — possibly "Advocati Curiae" (Advocate of the Court), a title
- The scalloped/wave decorative line separates the name from the date
- Two six-pointed star ornaments bracket the inscription
- This is an 18th-century provenance mark, not original manuscript text
