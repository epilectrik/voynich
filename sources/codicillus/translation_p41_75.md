## Summary of Key Technical Content (Pages 41-75)

### Fire Degrees and Heating Procedures

- **Natural fire** [Latin: ignis naturalis] vs. **common/artificial fire** [Latin: ignis communis] — a central distinction throughout (pp.59-62)
- **Debilitation** [Latin: debilitatio] = reducing the fire's intensity; **Fortification** [Latin: ofortatio] = increasing it (pp.61-67)
- Operations must stay **within the latitude of two degrees** [Latin: infra latitudinem duorum graduum] (p.61)
- **Balneum** (water bath) distillation explicitly mentioned (p.72) with gentle/constant heat
- **Boiling furnace** [Latin: furnus fervidus] referenced (p.75)

### Graduated Letter Sequences

The cipher alphabet encodes a ladder of operational stages:

| Stage | Letters | Operation |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| B. -> g. | c. d. e. f. | Descent from finished metals to first earths |
| g. -> m. | h. i. k. l. | From earths to raw principles (sulfur & mercury) |
| m. -> r. | n. o. p. | From principles to fluid mercury |
| r. -> y. | s. t. u. x. | From fluid mercury to ultimate perfection |

**Critical rule:** No stage can be skipped. B. cannot reach g. without passing through c.d.e.f. (pp.56-58)

### Color Sequence (Transmutation Signs)

1. **Nigredo** (blackness) — corruption stage
2. **Albedo** (whiteness) — purification
3. **Citrinus** (yellowness) — intermediate
4. **Rubedo** (redness) — final perfection

### Core Operations

Four principal parts of the mastery (p.66):
1. **Sublimation, calcination, dissolution, separation** of the stone
2. **Weighing, sublimatory/calcinatory rarefaction** of elements
3. **Softening** [Latin: lenificatio] and subtilizing the stone
4. **Sweetening** [Latin: dulcificatio], maturation, fermentation, animation, fixation

### Three Fundamental Operations (p.47)

- **Soluere** (dissolve)
- **Lauare** (wash)
- **Fixare** (fix)

### Practical Recipe Details (pp.71-73)

- Mercury sublimation with saltpeter [Latin: sal petri]
- Proportions: 1 part d. + 1/2 part c. of mercury
- Three drachms measure
- Water of gold of the sun [Latin: aqua auri solis]
- Three-day suspension periods
- Cucurbit + alembic distillation apparatus
- Balneum (water bath) with constant heat
- Goal: substance becomes "very white and crystalline"
- Subsequent rubification (reddening) phase
- Paulatim et paulatim (little by little) — graduated heating
