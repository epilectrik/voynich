## Page 30: Alphabetical Wheel [Latin: Rota Alphabetalis] (Lullian Wheels)

Layout: approximately 9 circular diagrams in a roughly 3x3 grid, all in red ink.

### Top Row — Three Figures
1. **First figure** [Latin: prima figura] — concentric circles, letters A. B. C. D., labels: "of the sign" [Latin: signalis], "of negation" [Latin: negationis], "figure" [Latin: figura]
2. **Second figure** [Latin: 2a figura] — concentric circles, letters A. B. C. D., label: "of affirmation" [Latin: affirmationis]
3. **Third part** [Latin: 3a pars] — with "God" [Latin: deus] labeled nearby

### Middle Row
4. **Large M** at center — Gothic capital M, outer ring letters B. S. A., "fourth figure" [Latin: quarta figura]
5. **Overlapping circles** (vesica piscis) — "active/passive" [Latin: activa/passiva], "matter" [Latin: materia], "workable" [Latin: fiabilis]
6. **Sectored circle** — "wisdom" [Latin: sapientia], "concord" [Latin: concordia], "matter" [Latin: materia]

### Bottom Row
7. **Circle with h** — "Circle of digestion" [Latin: circulus digestionis] labeled below. Cross symbol.
8. **Circle with Omega/U** — sectors with letters A. D., divided into compartments
9. **Small circle** — "Emmanuel" [Latin: ymmanuel], "good God" [Latin: bonus deus] — Christological/theological key

### Key Features
- All text in red ink — diagram-only page
- Theological terms (God, Emmanuel, Wisdom) mixed with natural philosophy (digestion, matter, humidity)
- Active/passive distinction in overlapping circles
- "Circle of digestion" explicitly identifies alchemical context
- Letter stations (A, B, C, D, S) at positions around wheels

### Page 32: Elemental Layers and Apparatus

**Concentric Circles (elementated forms):**
Post-distillation elemental composition:
- Outermost: processed water [Latin: aqua elementata]
- Second: processed earth [Latin: terra elementata]
- Third: processed air and wine [Latin: aer et vinum elementatum]
- Innermost: processed fire [Latin: ignis elementatus]

**Flask Drawing:**
- **"The master's alembic"** [Latin: lambicus magistri]
- Long neck with curved spout, round body with dots (material inside)

**Sulfur and Medicine Section:**
- Sulfur discussion continues
- **Medicine** [Latin: medicina] introduced as the goal
- **Gold** [Latin: aurum] referenced
- Two small circles at bottom with "red" [Latin: rubeum] and "gnano" labels

### Key Finding
The concentric elemental layers show a PHYSICAL MODEL of distillation:
fire at the core (the active principle), surrounded by earth, air, and water
in successive layers. This is what distillation progressively separates.

## Summary of Key Technical Content (Pages 41-75)

### Fire Degrees and Heating Procedures

- **Natural fire** [Latin: ignis naturalis] vs. **common/artificial fire** [Latin: ignis communis] — a central distinction throughout (pp.59-62)
- **Debilitation** [Latin: debilitatio] = reducing the fire's intensity; **Fortification** [Latin: ofortatio] = increasing it (pp.61-67)
- Operations must stay **within the latitude of two degrees** [Latin: infra latitudinem duorum graduum] (p.61)
- **Balneum** (water bath) distillation explicitly mentioned (p.72) with gentle/constant heat
- **Boiling furnace** [Latin: furnus fervidus] referenced (p.75)

### Graduated Letter Sequences

The cipher alphabet encodes a ladder of operational stages:

| Stage | Letters | Operation |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| B. -> g. | c. d. e. f. | Descent from finished metals to first earths |
| g. -> m. | h. i. k. l. | From earths to raw principles (sulfur & mercury) |
| m. -> r. | n. o. p. | From principles to fluid mercury |
| r. -> y. | s. t. u. x. | From fluid mercury to ultimate perfection |

**Critical rule:** No stage can be skipped. B. cannot reach g. without passing through c.d.e.f. (pp.56-58)

### Color Sequence (Transmutation Signs)

1. **Nigredo** (blackness) — corruption stage
2. **Albedo** (whiteness) — purification
3. **Citrinus** (yellowness) — intermediate
4. **Rubedo** (redness) — final perfection

### Core Operations

Four principal parts of the mastery (p.66):
1. **Sublimation, calcination, dissolution, separation** of the stone
2. **Weighing, sublimatory/calcinatory rarefaction** of elements
3. **Softening** [Latin: lenificatio] and subtilizing the stone
4. **Sweetening** [Latin: dulcificatio], maturation, fermentation, animation, fixation

### Three Fundamental Operations (p.47)

- **Soluere** (dissolve)
- **Lauare** (wash)
- **Fixare** (fix)

### Practical Recipe Details (pp.71-73)

- Mercury sublimation with saltpeter [Latin: sal petri]
- Proportions: 1 part d. + 1/2 part c. of mercury
- Three drachms measure
- Water of gold of the sun [Latin: aqua auri solis]
- Three-day suspension periods
- Cucurbit + alembic distillation apparatus
- Balneum (water bath) with constant heat
- Goal: substance becomes "very white and crystalline"
- Subsequent rubification (reddening) phase
- Paulatim et paulatim (little by little) — graduated heating

### Metals Hierarchy (p.54)

| Purity Level | Examples |
|--------------|----------|
| Purest | Sol (gold), Luna (silver) |
| Intermediate | Other metals |
| More corrupt | Marcasite, magnesia, tutia (zinc oxide) |
| Most corrupt | Salts, vitriols (atramenta), alums (alumina) |

### Dedication

- To the King of England, specifically King Edward (p.42)
- Edward swore a strict oath to protect the work
- Also addressed to "dearest son" [Latin: fili Carissime] (p.52) and "Henry" (p.43)

### Key Technical Content

**The Operational Wheel (Rota):**
- Pages 82-83 describe a cyclical "wheel" [Latin: rota] of operations
- Named operations in order: sublimation, distillation, imbibition, separation, fixation, reduction, corruption
- The cycle repeats with increasing subtlety at each revolution

**The Dragon and Lion Metaphors (p.86, p.95, p.98-99):**
- **The sulfur is identified as "the dragon devouring, the roaring lion" [Latin: draco devorans, leo rugiens]** (p.86)
- This sulfur is what makes copper [Latin: cuprum]; it mortifies and consumes, producing a rosy color
- The "hunting of lions" [Latin: venatio leonum] is praised as the beautiful ingenuity of the art (p.95)
- The sign is found "in the belly of the lion" [Latin: ventre leonis] (p.95)
- The **green lion** [Latin: leo viridis] is identified as the earth (p.98)
- The **devouring dragon** [Latin: draco devorans] is another name for the same earth (p.98)

**Putrefaction Procedures (pp.88-93, 96):**
- Putrefaction [Latin: putrefactio] is the essential prerequisite for all transformation
- The dissolved stone must putrefy to achieve the sulfurous mixture with watery silver
- Through putrefaction, the quinta essentia rises and results
- Natural humidity must be resolved; natural heat must first be corrupted
- The body's complexion must correct the argentum vivum
- Putrefaction strips away the stench [Latin: foetor] from the fetid earth

**Quinta Essentia (pp.88-90):**
- What arises from putrefaction "without doubt is called the quinta" (p.89)
- It possesses all operational virtue and is the cause of mixture
- Through it, the best transmuters can reduce bodies to their first matters [Latin: primas materias]
- Connected to the "spirit with the fifth principle" [Latin: re quinta] (p.90)
- The primordial matter [Latin: materia primordialis] has no form of its own (ancient teaching)
- A living agent [Latin: agens vita] uniformly corrupts matter toward the ultimate form

**Transmutation Operations (pp.76-86):**
- Repeated 5-day digestion cycles with sealed vessels
- Rarefaction through double distillation in the bath
- Moistening, liquefaction, vivification sequence for the rubified body
- Binding operations with measured water (two fingers' depth above material)
- The sulfurous spirit freed in heat, dissolved in cold (p.86)
- Fire of fires [Latin: ignis ignium] needed to achieve rubification (p.78)

**Argentum Vivum (Mercury) Doctrine (pp.87, 97):**
- Not common mercury [Latin: ar. vi. vulgare] (p.97)
- Not separated by common sublimation [Latin: sublimatione vulgari]
- Rather from a "luminous substance" [Latin: lumosa substantia]
- Its virtue and power transmute all elements
- References the Great Testament [Latin: Magnum Testamentum] as prior authority (p.87)

## Translator's Notes

### Color Sequence (the Great Work)
The text describes the classic alchemical color progression:
1. **Nigredo** (blackness) — putrefaction, the "death" of matter (pp. 135-136, 151-153)
2. **Albedo** (whiteness) — washing/purification, achieved through repeated ablution and trituration (pp. 143, 148, 150-155)
3. **Rubedo** (redness) — the final perfection, achieved by continued fire after whiteness is complete (pp. 150-151)

### Key Alchemical Operations Referenced
- **Dissolutio et coagulatio** (dissolve and coagulate) — the fundamental cycle (p. 156)
- **Calcinatio** — reduction to powder by heat (pp. 132, 135)
- **Sublimatio** — purification by vaporization and re-condensation (p. 142)
- **Assatio** — dry roasting (pp. 144, 146)
- **Digestio** — prolonged gentle heating (throughout)
- **Fixatio** — making the volatile permanent (pp. 137, 142)
- **Trituratione** — grinding and pulverizing (pp. 135, 143)
- **Ablutio terrae** — washing of the earth (p. 150)

### Body-Spirit Relationship
The text extensively discusses the interplay between *corpus* (body = fixed principle) and *spiritus* (spirit = volatile principle). The central doctrine: dissolution of the body IS the congelation of the spirit, and vice versa (p. 142). Neither must exceed the other in quantity.

### The Stone and Elixir
The *lapis* (stone/philosopher's stone) is described as passing through sulfurous forms before becoming *elixir* (pp. 138, 161-162). It is extracted from the "two luminaries" (gold and silver, p. 162) and must achieve both white and red forms.

### Manuscript Condition
This transcription contains extensive damage and abbreviation. Many passages are only partially recoverable; the [?] markers reflect genuine uncertainty in the source reading. The translation attempts to follow the sense of the argument even where individual words are uncertain.
