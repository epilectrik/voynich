# Codicillus Pages 14-20 — Apparatus Drawings + Early Text

## Page 14: Two Furnace Drawings

### Left: Tower Furnace (Athanor)
- Multi-tiered brick tower with arched fire door
- Labels: "fornus" (furnace), "alutel" (sublimation vessel), "fmus" (fumes)
- Top: small alembic head ("moriambici") shown separately

### Right: Athanor with Dome/Bell
- Larger furnace with sand bath containing flask
- Dome/bell cover over vessel on support ring
- Label: "athanor triplex" (triple athanor)

### Provenance note (bottom, different hand):
"Domino comes... noc[te] perfecit[us] Anno..."

## Page 15: Multi-Receiver Distillation Apparatus

Central cucurbit with alembic head, multiple side tubes to pear-shaped
receiver flasks on both sides. Large round-bottomed flask below.

Label inside large flask:
**"instrumentum magnum nomine pro rectificatione"**
(The great instrument for rectification)

This is a multi-fraction distillation setup for separating distillate
into multiple grades simultaneously — consistent with Lullian graduated
distillation practice and the operations encoded on f76r.

## Pages 16-20: Procedural Text

### Section Headers (red ink):
- p.16: "primo deinde modo sequenti et deinde proce..." (first then in following manner)
- p.16: "de modo serintandi... recipiendi primam constantem" (on receiving the first constant)
- p.18: "de modo prohibendi et indulco[?]andi" (on prohibiting and sweetening)
- p.19: "De ultimis digestionibus" (On the final digestions)
- p.20: "constolanto pro separatione perfecta" (for perfect separation)

### Content Notes:
