## Context

Hermann Schedel (c. 1410-1485) was a physician based in Augsburg. His nephew **Hartmann Schedel** (1440-1514), the more famous of the two, studied medicine at the University of Padua from 1463 to 1466, overlapping with the period covered by this correspondence. Hartmann Schedel assembled the largest known private library in 15th-century Germany, comprising approximately 370 manuscripts and 670 printed books. Much of this collection survives in the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich.

The correspondence is preserved primarily in Munich manuscript CLM 224, Hermann's recipe book, into which letter drafts, political reports, and other documents were interspersed. The edition was prepared by Paul Joachimsohn from this and related manuscripts (CLM 441, CLM 418, CLM 24847).
