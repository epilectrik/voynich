For the heart: Portulaca, Melissa, Bugloss

Hereafter follows a useful matter concerning many kinds of distilled waters, how one should use and apply them for the health of people. And this little book has been collected and written together from the books by Master Michael Schrick, Doctor of Medicine, out of love and at the request of honorable persons.

On Rose water.

This water is somewhat cold and very dry. This same distilled water is good for sick people who are faint from unnatural heat; one should apply it to them, and it strengthens the heart. It is good against the pestilence, and for those whose head is hot, and especially for redness, swelling, and diseases of the eyes. It is also good for a hot liver. Applied to the temples and pulse points, it relieves shooting pains and brings good restful sleep. And especially when one tempers it with chrism, it is good against fainting. It also stops the flux of dysentery in the belly, and cools the liver and the stomach. And it is good when one is spitting blood.

On roses that grow upon the hawthorn.

Roses that grow upon the hawthorn — this distilled water is of a warm nature and is good for those who are of a cold constitution. And for those whose stomach has grown cold, one should give it to them to consume. And whatever sickness a person has on account of coldness, it is good for that.

On corn poppy water.

Red poppies or corn poppies that grow among the grain — this water is good enough for all internal diseases that come from watery heat, especially for the liver when it is full of unnatural heat. Drunk, it helps against convulsions in young or old people. And especially it is good for St. Anthony's fire, applied externally and drunk. Also, women who have their monthly sickness too heavily should drink it. Also, a small cloth soaked in it and placed between the pulse points stops their monthly flow. Also, a cloth soaked and laid upon the liver stops the bleeding

of the nose. And it is good for all sores and ulcers in the mouth and on the lips.

On white lily water.

White lily water is good for the eyes — dropped in and applied around them after bathing. It draws out the heat and redness so that they become clear and do not swell. And especially, the face washed with it makes it bright, fair, and clear. It is also good when a person is faint and feverish around the heart and around the liver. And it is good for those who have bad humors and who feel tightness around the heart on account of evil heat.

On blue lily water.

Blue lily water is good for all fevers of whatever kind they may be. It is also good to drink for red dropsy, and liver disease, and jaundice, and for intestinal colic, and griping in the body, and old wounds, and cancer, and for Noli me tangere — it heals that very well.

On speckled lily or wild lily water.

Speckled lilies that grow among the thorns — this water is good for heart constriction and tightness in the chest. It is good for those with dropsy, and for those whose lungs are rotting or have sores upon them, and who wheeze badly. It produces long breath and clears the chest. It is good for those who have stones in the loins and cleanses the kidneys, and it is good against unchastity. Also, it is good for one who fears becoming leprous, and for those who are red and swollen under the eyes. It makes the face beautiful and smooth, and it is good when a stroke or palsy has struck someone. And for those whose limbs are paralyzed or withered, they should rub themselves with it, and it will help them.

On lily of the valley water.

Lilium Convallium — that is, lily of the valley water.

--- Page 16 ---

It is good for anyone who has consumed poison; one should give it to them to drink at once. And if a spider or a poisonous animal or worm has caused contamination, or a mad dog has bitten someone, if they drink this water it helps them. It is good for women when they labor in childbirth. And it is good for all impure diseases, and especially for the eyes — it clears them. It strengthens the brain and the heart and fortifies the senses. It is good for those who have the falling sickness, and for those who fear leprosy, and for women's diseases. And for those who have lost their speech, one applies it upon the tongue.

On violet water.

Violet water is good for the mad and for those who have lost their senses from foul and evil smell or smoke. Also, this water soaked in a linen cloth and laid upon warts drives them away. Also, it cools the heart naturally and quenches thirst. And it is also good for people whose eyes are about to swell. And it is useful and good for those who have heart-sting, for it cools and strengthens the entire body gently.

On yellow wallflower water.

Yellow wallflower water is good for those who have a severe sore throat — it strengthens them. It fortifies the liver and the kidneys and makes women fertile, and cleanses them after childbirth. It is also good when the brain is sick from coldness — it warms it and strengthens the senses. Also, it strengthens the memory and produces good sense, and gladdens the soul and also the spirits. Also, it clarifies and strengthens corrupted blood and warms the marrow in the knees and drives out all cold diseases. It is also good for those whom palsy or

a stroke has struck, so that their tongue is lame and they cannot speak — when one drinks it and rubs the side well with it. Also, for those whose hands tremble, they should anoint their hands with it (but not drink it), and it takes away the trembling. Also, it inwardly cools the heart and gladdens the blood. Also, it is good for all blemishes that are on the face, whether from heat or from cold, and makes the face white, bright, and clear. A cloth soaked in it and bound around the head is good and relieves all aching and shooting pains of the head, and brings good sleep.

On water lily water.

Water lilies — the white ones with the broad leaves that float upon the lakes. This distilled water is very useful and good for the red blotches under the eyes that come from heat, for it draws all heat into itself. It also makes the skin white and powerfully cools the heart and the liver. Also, it quenches all fevers on the human body with certainty.

On larkspur water.

Distilled larkspur water is very useful and good for all redness and swelling of the eyes. For when one soaks a cloth in it and lays it over the eyes, all redness goes out of the eyes and makes them clear, and it also prevents swelling. It is good for all pressure in the body and for intestinal colic. And for those who cannot keep their food down. Also, it drives the urinary stone.

On bugloss water.

Bugloss water is good in the same manner as rosemary water. It is also very good for scurf of the head. And especially it is very good for women against their diseases and sicknesses.

On borage water.

--- Page 18 ---

Borage water is good in every way like rosemary blossom water. Also, it is especially good for palsy and for all fluxes that come from the head.

On linden blossom water.

Linden blossom water is good for children who have the falling sickness — give it to them to drink and they will recover. It is also good for the womb and trembling of the heart, and for the stone. Also, it is good when the hands or feet are frostbitten, and it draws the coldness out. Also, it is good for all diseases that come from coldness, and especially for women when the womb has grown cold.

On plantain water.

Broad plantain water is good for dysentery, when one gives it to drink with Armenian clay or with the stone amethyst. Then it stops that same flux and also stops women's monthly flow. The narrow-leafed plantain is good for wounds — when one washes them with it, it cleanses them and heals them.

On sorrel water.

Sorrel water is good for all hot conditions. For it cools and quenches all hot diseases, whether internal or external. It cools the heat of the head and also of the liver and all members of the body. And especially St. Anthony's fire. And also the pestilence.

On nightshade water.

Nightshade water cools and drives away all heat, external or internal, of the body. And especially whatever hot abscesses or sores there are, or whatever hot swelling there is — drunk and applied with a cloth upon the head. For it powerfully cools all things.

On hart's-tongue fern water.

Hart's-tongue water is hot and dry. And it is exceptionally good for the spleen. Indeed, the spleen sometimes becomes so hard in the body, like a stone, and cracks loudly so that one can clearly hear it, as if a chip were breaking. For this, one should drink this water for forty days, and it will certainly help. It is also good for hiccups, and it breaks up the stone in the loins and also in the bladder. I, Michael Schrick, have often proven that it is very good for severe shuddering and fearful dreams that often come from malice of the spleen. Likewise for melancholy and sadness that also come from the spleen and black bile. Note: one may well mix it with wild savin water in equal parts, for they are both very good for all ailments and malice of the spleen.

On houseleek water.

Houseleek water cools greatly. And it is similar to stonecrop. Yet it is much colder in itself, and neither of them is good for a person to drink internally.

On couch-grass water.

Couch-grass water is good for the bloody flux. Also, it quenches St. Anthony's fire. And when a person has bad blisters, if one binds it over them, it helps very well.

On self-heal water.

Self-heal water is good for the blessed sickness and for convulsions in young and old people. Also, it is good against the pestilence, and especially for sores and putrefaction in the mouth, and whatever comes from evil unnatural heat — it cools it.

On fumitory or centaury water.

Fumitory water, or chervil water, is good for people who fear leprosy. And it is good —

drunk, it dries out all scabies and mange from the body and then heals it. And otherwise, if one takes a little theriac and a good drink of it before going into the bath, one sweats out all disease and poison. And one should not pour water over oneself until one has sweated thoroughly. This drives away all scurf and scabs, internal and external. It also cleanses women's diseases. When they use it, as they have need of it, it helps very well.

On saxifrage water.

This herb is said to be very good for gravel or sand. The herb is shaped at the bottom with small leaves like a rose, and has long red stems upon the leaves, and at the top of the tips also a few small leaves. It is also good for the urinary stone — it breaks it up.

On shallot water.

Shallot water is very hot, like onion. And it has a bad moisture. And it is bad for the eyes if one uses too much of it. It induces sleep and consumes much moisture in the stomach. Therefore one drinks it for worms and for griping.

On mallow water.

Mallow water is cold and moist — more moist than cold — and is good for softening all hard things that are hot. For it moistens very well, internally and externally. It is good for sleep and for hot fever, and for desiccation when one washes the legs with it. And it is also good for wiping the hip area. Likewise, great rose-hip water has the same virtue.

On mugwort / maiden's girdle / red buck water.

Mugwort or maiden's girdle water is hot and dry in the second degree. And it is good for scabs on the scalp. And it makes the beard grow in good time. And it strengthens the

chest and the cold stomach. And it is good against worms. And it brings on women's monthly flow and urine. And it drives away gravel and breaks the stone. And it is good against gout. And the herb drives away all poisonous vermin when one boils it and sprinkles the rooms of the houses with it. And drunk lukewarm, it drives away womb disorders. And it is good against jaundice and against swelling of the liver and the spleen.

On asparagus water.

Asparagus water is equally hot and dry. And the water brings on urine as strongly as the urine smells of it afterward. And therefore it is good for gravel, and it expels the matter from which gravel forms. And it is good for intestinal colic. It also expels the obstruction of the liver and the spleen from the loins. And if one wishes to expel the common stone and the gravel, one should not mix this water with anything else.

Item: for womb disorders, make a fumigation of frankincense and place it on a glowing coal, and hold the mouth over it and let it enter well into you.

On dill water.

Dill water is equally hot and dry. And it is good for moisture of the teeth and of the chest. For it makes much milk in wet-nurses. And it is good for the stomach. And it brings on urine and destroys the gravel.

On eyebright water.

Eyebright water is good in wintertime for all manner of ailments that are in the eyes, and it wards them off.

On valerian water.

Valerian water, which one distills together with the roots — this water one should drink against poisoning and against the bites of venomous worms, if one rubs it on the area over the heart. And it is good against quartan fever. Against the pain

On willow blossom water.

Willow blossom water is good for the eyesight. The hair also becomes beautiful from it. And the filth from the head is driven away by it.

On agrimony blossom water.

Agrimony blossom water is very good against all swelling, internal and external, if one drinks it or wets a small cloth in it and lays it folded double over the affected part where the swelling is external. And against gout there is nothing better than drinking this water. Also, whoever has running eyes for a long time, let him put one or two drops in the eyes for several days — it will certainly improve. It is good against all ailments that come from fluxes, of whatever kind they may be. Item: internally one should drink it; externally, anoint with it. This water is cold and of a drying nature. I, Michael Schrick, have often proven this.

On fraxinella water.

Fraxinella water is good for young children when unnatural heat overcomes and sickens them — give it to them to drink and it will pass. Also it is good for those who are short of breath around the heart and the chest — it ventilates and helps very well. Also it is good for old or young people who have swelling around the heart or the chest or in the lungs — it drives it away. And it is good against all evil things.

On strawberry water.

Strawberry water is good for whoever has impure, foul sweat, and internal evil heat, and it quenches thirst and drives the stone. And it makes good blood when drunk with wine or otherwise eaten. It is also good for those who have ulceration in the mouth. Also those who have a sore

It is also good for people who lie unconscious — if one gives it to them to consume, it helps very well. Also it is good for the head and for the heart, for it strengthens them well. Also it is very good for women for their menstrual complaints. Also it is good against wasting. It is also good for people who have a broken bone — it heals it, and the bone grows back together, and also all wounds washed with it, it heals them. And whoever is wounded internally in the body, one should give this to him to drink.

On mouse-ear water.

Mouse-ear water is good against wasting when drunk. It also stops womb disorders. It is also good against intestinal colic. And against dizziness in the head. And against spitting blood. Also it is good against all blemishes under the eyes if one washes with it and does not drink it — then it drives away the blemishes. And it is good for women whose womb rises upward and who become ill and have griping around the navel. And it kills the worm in the belly immediately.

On southernwood water.

Southernwood water is good for young children against heartburn and against griping in the belly. It is also good for children who have womb-troubles, and also it is good against headache.

On shepherd's-purse water.

Shepherd's-purse water is good drunk for one who bleeds from the nose. And it is good against the red and white dysentery, and especially for women who have their complaints too heavily, or who otherwise have flux of the bowels. And it is good for the stone — it expels it.

On spindle-tree water (or priest's-cap water).

Spindle-tree is hot and dry, and it is good for abscesses in the bladder and in the kidneys, and for hot sores, and it expels all bites. And it is very good against jaundice, for it expels the swelling through the urine in a full bath. If one cannot obtain the water, then boil the root in wine and drink it in a full bath.

On comfrey water.

Comfrey water is not good for any person to drink, for it is poisonous. But otherwise, well rubbed in, it expels gout. And it quenches St. Anthony's fire and reduces all swelling wherever a person has it.

On radish water.

Radish water is good against the passing stone. Also it kills the worms in the stomach. And it brings on digestion. And it produces urine. And it expels gravel. Horseradish does the same.

On carrot water.

Carrot water is good for the passing stone and for the bladder stone. Also, if the water is salted and warmed a little and drunk on an empty stomach, and held for a short time, and then a feather dipped in oil is thrust into the throat and stirred — this carries out the cold, stagnant moisture and the watery bile from which tertian and quartan fevers come, and it expels them.

On wild sage water.

Wild sage water is good and beneficial against the stabbing pain in the heart from bad blood. Also it is good and useful for people who have been beaten or struck or wounded so that they are bruised. And the blood that has clotted within them, it breaks it apart and expels it from the person's body.

On chamomile water.

Chamomile water is hot and dry. And it is quite naturally suited to the human complexion. And it is good for all fatigue of all limbs, and it strengthens the limbs greatly. And it drives on the urine. And it cleanses the fever when one anoints the sick person with it. And it is good for women's complaints, for it brings on menstruation.

On parsley water.

Parsley water is good against kidney stones — it breaks them and expels them. It also cleanses the liver and helps very well for those who cannot pass urine.

On valerian water.

Valerian water: it is good when a person wets a cloth in it after the bath and lays it over the eyes — that makes the eyes bright and clear from the heat and smoke of the bath. Also, this water drunk and applied to fig-warts drives them away in both men and women, and drunk it helps when something is ruptured or otherwise broken — it heals it greatly. Also drunk in the morning on an empty stomach before one goes out of the house, it is good against the smoke and stench of the pestilence.

On betony water.

Betony water is very good and useful against all ailments in the head that come from heat. With a cloth bound around the head, it drives away the pain and also the shooting pains in the head. Also it is good to drink for the overheated stomach and liver — it cools them and heals them surely. Also it is good for women who labor in childbirth. And it also cleanses their menstrual complaints. Also it heals all poisonous bites and the raging of beasts and mad dogs.

On rue blossom water.

Rue blossom water diminishes women's excessive menstrual flow in those who have it too heavily — it stops it and strengthens the heart.

On southernwood water (Abrauter or Gertel).

Southernwood or Gertel water is hot and dry. And it is good against shortness of breath that comes from cold. And for the cold stomach. It consumes the raw matter in the stomach or in the intestines from which mucus grows. And the worms in the stomach. Also it is good against gout and breaks the stone and brings on the urine.

On fennel water.

Fennel water is hot and dries very strongly. And it is good for the eyes. And when nature goes from the lungs or the back, it runs to the fennel. Also it is good for obstructions of the spleen. And when the belly swells, it purges it and brings on the urine.

On elderflower water.

Elderflower water is hot and dry. And it promotes the liver and makes the face beautiful. And it drives away blemishes and protects the eyes from the heat of the sun, and helps against headache at the back of the neck when a wet cloth is laid on and rubbed in. And it prevents leprosy and promotes the stomach.

On blue cornflower water.

Blue cornflower water destroys the films over the eyes and makes the eyesight clear. And red eyes become beautiful from it.

Item: whoever has lost his senses, let him take plantain, southernwood, and sage, and pour distilled wine over them, and let it stand a day and a night; then drink of it for five days in a row, and the person will come back to his senses.

On distilled wine (brandy).

Distilled wine is also good for people whose head aches. Whoever washes his head with it is always handsome and young for a long time, and it makes a good memory, for distilled wine strengthens a person's senses and wits. Whoever washes his face with it does not go gray. It also kills nits and mites. And whoever has foul breath, let him rub it on and drink a little with other wine, and he will have sweet breath.

Item: whoever has a cough, let him drink distilled wine with other wine and he will become healthy.

Also whoever has cloudy and red eyes, let him rub a little on the brows, and when he goes to sleep, let him drip drops into the eyes, and he will become healthy.

Item: whoever cannot hear, let him drip a drop into the ears, and he will hear.

Also whoever is dropsical, let him drink distilled wine and rub it around the belly when he is about to leave the bath, by a fire, and he will feel better.

Whoever also has earworms or other insects in the ears — they will die from the wine.
