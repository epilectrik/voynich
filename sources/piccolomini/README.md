## Context

Aeneas Silvius Piccolomini (1405-1464), later Pope Pius II, served as secretary in the imperial chancellery of Frederick III in Vienna from January 1443. His letters from this period document:

- The political and intellectual environment of Vienna and the Habsburg court
- The Council of Basel (where he served before coming to Vienna)
- Humanist networks in central Europe
- Imperial diplomacy and chancellery operations
- His description of Vienna itself (Letter 55, c. May 1443)

The primary manuscript source is Munich clm. 12725, written 1443-1446 under Piccolomini's supervision by Ludwig Scheyter, a royal chancery official. Wolkan identified two later redactions by Piccolomini himself: one in 1447 (standardizing pronouns, removing the "poeta" title) and a later revision removing erotically suggestive passages and politically sensitive statements as his ecclesiastical career advanced.

Volume 1 contains 321 letters and is the most relevant for the Vienna arrival period.

## OCR Quality

The text was digitized by Google Books and OCR'd for Internet Archive. Quality is generally readable but has typical OCR artifacts from a 1909 German/Latin scholarly edition:

- Occasional character substitutions (especially in apparatus criticus)
- Some garbled text at page boundaries and in footnotes
- Ligatures and special characters may be misread
- Page headers and numbers are interspersed in the text

## Relevance

Piccolomini's Vienna correspondence (1443-1454) is contemporary with the period of interest for Voynich Manuscript provenance studies. His letters provide primary documentation of the intellectual and political milieu at the Habsburg court during the mid-15th century.
