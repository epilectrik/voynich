## Key Persons Documented

- **Michael Puff von Schrick** (also: Michahel Puff de Schrick/Srikch/Schrichk) -- Dean of the Medical Faculty 11 times between 1435-1470. Senior member ("senior noster"). References throughout the Acta documenting his elections, faculty meetings, disputations, and administrative decisions.
- **Johannes Tichtel** (also: Joannes Tichtell ex Greyn/Grein) -- Doctor of arts and medicine, multiple terms as Dean. Documented from the 1470s-1490s.
- **Johannes de Ketham** -- Medical faculty member, author of *Fasciculus Medicinae*.

## Context

Official records of the Vienna University Medical Faculty (Wiener medicinische Facultät). These are the decanal acts -- minutes kept by the Dean during his term of office, recording:

- Faculty meetings (congregationes) and decisions
- Dean elections (held twice yearly, summer and winter semesters)
- Student examinations, promotions, and degree conferrals (bachelor, licentiate, doctor)
- Financial accounts (income from fees, expenditures)
- Disputes with apothecaries and unlicensed practitioners
- Anatomical demonstrations
- Property management (the faculty house)
- Disciplinary actions and statutes

This is the institution where Michael Puff von Schrick worked for over 35 years, serving as the most frequently elected Dean in the faculty's medieval history. The records provide direct documentary evidence of his activities, colleagues, and the institutional environment of 15th-century Viennese medicine.

## OCR Quality Notes

The text was digitized by Google from a Harvard University copy. OCR quality is generally good for the German editorial text but variable for the Latin manuscript transcription, particularly:
- Ligatures and special characters (ae, oe) sometimes misread
- Footnote markers occasionally garbled
- Some line-break artifacts in the OCR
- Caret/hat symbols (^) sometimes appear where commas or other punctuation should be

An alternative scan from the Wellcome Library (identifier: `b29001869_0001`) provides slightly cleaner OCR for the Latin text but covers only Volumes 1-2.
