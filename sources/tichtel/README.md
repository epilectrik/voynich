# Johannes Tichtel's Diary (Tagebuch), 1477-1495

## Source

- **URL:** https://archive.org/stream/fontesrerumaustr01kaisuoft/fontesrerumaustr01kaisuoft_djvu.txt
- **Full volume:** https://archive.org/details/fontesrerumaustr01kaisuoft

## Publication Details

- **Title:** Fontes Rerum Austriacarum, Erste Abtheilung: Scriptores, Band I
- **Editor:** Theodor Georg von Karajan
- **Publisher:** Kaiserlich-Konigliche Hof- und Staatsdruckerei, Vienna
- **Year:** 1855 (MDCCCLV)
- **Series:** Published by the Historische Commission der Kaiserlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften in Wien

## Original Manuscript

- **Repository:** Osterreichische Nationalbibliothek (ONB), Vienna
- **Shelfmark:** Inc. III.A.5 (Karajan references it as III.A.3; the shelfmark may have been updated)
- **Physical description:** Tichtel wrote his diary entries in the flyleaves and parchment strips of a printed copy of Avicenna's Canon, Book III (Padua edition, 1472, Hain 2213). The diary is written in small, heavily abbreviated script across:
  - Front: a 14-inch x 6-inch parchment strip and a paper leaf of equal height (10 inches wide)
  - Back: the verso of the last printed leaf plus two additional leaves of equal size
  - Additional fragments found later under the front cover binding

## Date Range

1477-1495 (with some entries possibly extending to 1494 based on the final entries in the manuscript).

## Language

Latin with occasional German words and phrases. The text is a mix of:
- Daily income records (patient fees in Hungarian florins, Rhenish florins, librae, solidi, denarii)
- Personal events (family, health, household purchases)
- Historical observations (siege of Vienna 1485, Hungarian occupation, Maximilian I)
- University life (lectures, disputations, decanal duties at the medical faculty)

## Volume Structure

The Fontes Rerum Austriacarum, Band I contains four works:

1. **Johannes Tichtel's Tagebuch, 1477-1495** (pp. 1-66)
2. Sigmund von Herberstein's Selbstbiographie, 1486-1553
3. Johannes Cuspinian's Tagebuch, 1502-1527
4. Georg Kirchmair's Denkwurdigkeiten, 1519-1553

The extracted file (`tichtel_diary_1477_1495.txt`) contains:
- Karajan's introduction specific to Tichtel (pp. VII-XII of the volume preface)
- The complete diary text (pp. 1-61)
- Karajan's postscript (pp. 61-66), including:
  - Editorial note on the end of the main diary
  - A fragment of an earlier diary/account book (dated to 1472) found under the binding
  - A letter from Tichtel to Conrad Celtis (dated Feast of St. Dorothy [6 Feb] 1493)

## About Johannes Tichtel

Johannes Tichtel (also Tichtl) was a physician from Grein (Upper Austria) active in Vienna during the late 15th century. Key biographical facts from the diary:

- Obtained his medical degree and practiced in Vienna
- Held the position of Deacon of the Medical Faculty at the University of Vienna (multiple terms)
- Lectured on Avicenna's Canon at the university
- Lived through the Hungarian siege and occupation of Vienna (1485-1490)
- His brother Bartholomaeus Tichtel was a licentiate in theology and Rector of the University of Vienna

## Notes on the OCR Text

The text was obtained from Archive.org's DjVu OCR of the 1855 printed edition. As with any OCR of 19th-century Fraktur typesetting of medieval Latin, expect:
- OCR artifacts (misread characters, especially with the long-s used throughout)
- The original manuscript used heavy abbreviations; Karajan expanded most but noted uncertainties with dots (...)
- Karajan added punctuation and capitalization (the original manuscript had none)
- Footnotes by Karajan appear inline, marked with `*)` or numbered superscripts
- Page numbers from the original edition appear as standalone numbers on their own lines
