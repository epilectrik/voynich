Distillation Notes - Joe's Mint Run
====================================

Date: 2026-04-10
Setup: 2L flat bottom flask, aluminum pan, thin sand layer, hot plate (1500W)
Material: Fresh mint, ~200g packed into flask

Notes:
- Hot plate got hot enough to crack the ceramic plate underneath it in half
- Flask doing fine in the sand bath
- Clevenger trap pooling past the spout due to surface tension, then gravity-clearing in surges
- May need to adjust angle of rig for consistent flow
- Drip rate unreliable as control signal due to surface tension modifying drip size/timing
- Plan: use load cell (fluid weight over time) instead of drip counter for automated rig control loop
- Overfilled with water — mint floated to top, vigorous boil pushes liquid directly into condenser
- Lowered heat, letting it boil down before adding boiled-over water back in
- LESSON: fill water only 1/2 to 2/3 of flask max, leave headspace for mint float and foam
- Mint compacted significantly once heated — could have fit ~300g instead of 200g
- LESSON: pack more mint, less water next time
- Steam distillation (mint in separate flask above boiling water) produced very dark water
  that stained ceramic boiling beads
- Hydrodistillation (mint directly in boiling water) surprisingly less dark
- Investigating why steam extraction is more aggressive than direct boil
- Zero oil collected — likely yield too low (200g mint = 1-2mL max) AND
  volatiles condensing early on flask walls/neck and dripping back into boiling water
- TODO: insulate flask neck and path to condenser (foil wrap), reduce condenser cooling,
  use more plant material (2-3kg for visible oil yield)
