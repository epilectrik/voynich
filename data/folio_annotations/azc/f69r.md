> **Ring order RESOLVED 2026-06-01** against the IVTFF primary source
> (`reference/ZL_official.txt`, f69r): the transcriber labels the 16-segment
> ring (43 tokens) the **"Large circle"** (`@L0`, outer) and the continuous
> ring (11 tokens) the **"Small circle"** (`@Cc`, inner). Large encloses small
> ⇒ **C = OUTER, S = INNER**. This matches `currier_AZC.md`; an earlier version
> of this file had S/C swapped (S labelled outer). Corrected here.
> **Parse remap gotcha:** the parse mapped IVTFF `@L0`(large)→**C** and
> `@Cc`(small)→**S** — the placement letter does NOT track the IVTFF locus
> letter (`@Cc` became **S**, not C). This is the channel through which the
> swap originally entered.
